## 1. The product and its boundary

For each **located lexical reading**—a particular word, part of speech, and sense—the builder emits the definition that GSE's fixed signed prime basis can express, with a mask and a derivation record. This is a word's *definition*, not its whole meaning. Grammar, discourse, world identity and facts, and Regenesis story inference remain separate inputs to meaning. `dog` and `cat` can legitimately have near-identical definitional coordinates while Sparse Wiki and context keep their referents distinct. The builder must not smuggle a referent's reputation, emotion association, or encyclopedic fact into a lexical sign. (F046, F055–F057, F059, F062)

Correctness has two equal sides. A false sign distorts the definition; omitting a warranted component distorts it too. A sparse result is correct only when its sparsity is justified. The output must report source absence, unresolved reading identity, basis-inexpressible content, conflict, and proven silence separately. Neither caution nor model fluency substitutes for this two-sided test. (F045–F047, F059, F066)

**A source observation is not automatically definitional content.** F115 gives the concrete failure: *furious* may be associated with disgust without definitionally requiring disgust. In the inspected records, USAS supplies `E3-`; NRC supplies `anger=1` and `disgust=1`. Those are different assertions with different contracts. Neither their concurrence nor a stable corpus correlation converts an association into a necessary component. A rule must explain why its premises constrain the word's own definition. NRC observations can help investigate a disagreement or discriminate an already warranted residual; they cannot independently create a prime, a signed pole, or silence. This applies equally to a rating of a referent and a property of an argument.

### Two different uncertainty spaces

The builder reduces uncertainty about **which definition vector is warranted**. A complex word often has several prime components, so more independent and combined constraints can identify its vector faster than the vector of an axiomatic prime word. That does **not** mean its use is easier to interpret. A richer vector can connect to more causal, emotional, social, temporal and world-grounded inference chains. Some of those chains can remain simultaneously plausible to different interlocutors. Definition resolution and downstream interpretation can therefore move in opposite directions. GSE must record the former without claiming the latter is settled; Regenesis and context handle the latter. This is the distinction Rohan made in F087. *Significance Weighting* is a source of insight about conditional coverage and inferential branching, not a borrowed numerical theorem for this lexicon.

## 2. What is atomic, and what is smaller than an atom

**Primes are the smallest stable lexical contents in this representation.** A prime reading is hand-reviewed, like a letter in an alphabet. Its name or written exponent alone is insufficient: that spelling may also name other senses. Goddard's point in F091 is that the prime's **basic combinatorial properties help identify which sense is primitive**. The atomic record must therefore bind a located lexical unit to its coordinate/sign **and its combinatorial profile**: its semantic and syntactic role, permitted argument slots and valencies, documented combinations and exclusions, and the source that warrants each. It is not inferred from WordNet, a dictionary gloss, or a model. The current stored GSE schema names 72 primes in eight banks and identifies 14 opposite-pole pairs, leaving 58 canonical coordinates. This is **GSE's** basis, not a claim that NSM research specifies exactly these names or this HDC encoding. The basis and its mapping to NSM research must be versioned and reviewed before a bulk build. (F072–F076, F083, F088, F091)

**`NUMBER` is not a 73rd lexical prime or bank.** All 72 lexical primes have literal word exponents and intrinsic definitional content; `NUMBER` has none in any bank. The 73-entry computational registry in `gse/primitives_v2.py` must not be read as a later, competing lexical ontology. The 72-prime inventory and the registry's additional `NUMBER` operation are simultaneously valid at different levels. The 14 antonym pairs quotient the 72 lexical names to 58 signed coordinates. `NUMBER` then supplies hierarchical scale to a representation already made from those coordinates; it adds no coordinate. This is Rohan's explicit correction in F096–F098, and any code or document that calls `NUMBER` a lexical prime is wrong about the design.

**Relations among banks are the smallest construction operations.** They say which coordinate states can coexist, which require another bank or role, and which exclude another value. They are more basic to the *builder* than a word's prime vector, yet they are not extra lexical contents or extra coordinates. An alphabet has letters and rules for forming valid combinations; the rules do not become additional letters. Likewise, a 58-coordinate representation can have a much smaller lawful subset of the full Cartesian product. Linear independence of basis directions does not imply independence of admissible word definitions. This resolves the apparent contradiction in F088.

Goddard's account, which Rohan located in F090–F091, names a closely related three-part structure: **semantic primes and their associated grammar**, plus **semantic molecules and semantic templates**. A prime and its basic combinatorial properties form one account of the intended primitive sense; the grammar is not an optional repair added after a bare sign. GSE's bank/role relations are closest to the *construction function* of that grammar: they restrict legal combinations but have no independent lexical vector. The relationship is an analogy, not an identity of implementations. A **molecule** is on the composed-content side of a prime—a reusable complex meaning made of simpler content, potentially nested. A **template** is a reusable shape for a family of definitions. GSE can study these to write and test relational laws and derived macros; it must not relabel a rule as a prime or assume an NSM molecule is already one of GSE's signed coordinates. A molecule may entail some prime components while retaining content this basis cannot express; that remainder is not evidence that its other prime cells are silent. [Goddard 2012](https://doi.org/10.1515/ling-2012-0022) supplies the research framing; F088–F091 supply the intended GSE construction.

There are two levels of relation:

- **Exact identity:** the 14 opposite-pole aliases are one signed coordinate, not two observations. `BAD:+1` is `GOOD:−1` **at the coordinate level**. The two named lexical-unit records can still have separately documented combinatorial profiles; a signed alias does not merge their spellings, uses, or sense guards. The coordinate quotient applies before any source fact and never counts twice. It is the simplest relation Rohan describes in F076.
- **Conditional bank laws:** under a proved reading/role guard, a state in one or several banks removes impossible states elsewhere. A single prime can trigger one such law. Several established components can trigger several laws **and** a new law requiring their combination. Unlike the alias identity, a law can leave two possibilities, apply to one slot but not another, or be inapplicable for an unaligned reading. (F073, F076, F088)

The atomic reading is hand-defined as a **lexical unit plus coordinate/sign plus combinatorial profile**, with an explicit claim of what that reading does and does not define. The profile both distinguishes the prime sense from a homographic ordinary sense and supplies reviewed constraints for closure. A reusable constraint is separately recorded in the bank-law registry with its valid scope; a property documented for one exponent is not automatically a universal law. F148 retires the one-cell declaration: “The degenerate and wrong approach would be to just have a single +1 representation in a bank and write it off thus”, and not even the modern V “would be encodable with just the ‘V’ bank filled in.” What made that declaration degenerate was its 57 unexamined silences, not its single +1 (F151, agreed at F152). A prime's profile therefore records an **authored decision for every one of the 58 coordinates**, each with its authority (founder, NSM or GSE convention), location and warrant: its own pole; the companions its content requires, such as INTENSITY +1 on a prime the chart grades with VERY (F125–F126; row 5, F144); explicit absences, such as the head record's zeros (below) and the evaluator/descriptor exclusion (F067); and an **authored definition boundary** that declares every remaining coordinate silent. The examination is recorded line by line from the prime's chart box, judging each line a combination, a degree domain, a prohibition or a component, and each judged line is typed by a claim of its kind in the profile: a permitted combination, the degree domain or the prohibited construction. The chart presents only some of a prime's combinatorial possibilities, so the judged lines are consistent with the boundary without proving it. The boundary is the author's decision; reconstruction checks its consistency with the rules, not its semantic completeness (F159). Combinability is not content: “I want…” puts no WANT into I, and “feel something good” no FEEL into GOOD.

Content and explicit absence are rebuilt from typed claims and shared rules, not copied from the desired vector. Reconstruction checks both value and independently rooted provenance. Every cited law must be active, cover the decision's coordinates and force the decided value. A permissive relation earns no credit; redundant independently forcing laws remain credited. The rooted check existentially erases every target coordinate from every active relation, then asks the ordinary joint-support solver whether any independently supported outside assignment still permits a wrong target value. Thus two decisions cannot cite each other's final values around an equality cycle as their own proof. An unrooted attribution, independent-background conflict or proof solver limit receives its own named refusal rather than becoming proof.

A reconstruction lists only compiled claims as premises and records withheld claims separately. Only an examined authored boundary compiles directly to silence laws, guarded by the identified lexical unit; removing a decision or source claim reopens cells whose independent support disappears. Draft boundaries remain unapplied proposals. These are generic schema behaviors, not a claim that the current basis still consists of the first checkpoint's examined subset and remaining drafts. The current whole-basis result is the `prime-proof` receipt in the execution packet. Contradictions remain visible foundation conflicts, and ordinary homographs never inherit a prime's boundary.

**Head conventions are scoped, not a universal noun table.** The eight substantive coordinates are I, YOU, SOMEONE, SOMETHING, PEOPLE, BODY, KIND and PART. The one-hot convention applies only to an identified substantive **prime profile** (`lexical_unit_kind = PRIME`): its own head is +1 and the other seven are 0. Authored modifier, predicate, operator and time/place contracts may establish head zeros under their own guards; noun POS alone never establishes those guards. Person, thing, time and place family typing does not automatically write a parent coordinate: no SOMEONE in I, WHEN in NOW or WHERE in HERE.

Ordinary nouns follow the located target contracts in `nouns.py` through the same `ReadingSeed` constructor. A container or animal target can establish SOMETHING +1 and KIND 0; a human target SOMEONE +1; a sourced human collective PEOPLE +1; a category target KIND +1. Explicit part targets establish PART +1 and, when not a category, KIND 0. They do not inherit SOMETHING merely from physical-entity typing, and BODY or the possessor remains an unbound relation argument rather than the part's head. Whole-body, collective-body, leadership-head and anatomical-head readings remain separate. No ordinary noun receives the prime one-hot zeros.

These are labeled sourced GSE projection conventions. Reviewed nominal frame/FE identities and complete, located LU-definition claims supply the guard; incorporated FEs and raw DEN valences are preserved as native evidence, not assumed to identify a unique lexical head. A container's Contents/Type/Part, clothing's Wearer/Body_location, and a teaching frame's participants cannot leak into the noun's own cells. Unknown target contracts produce named unresolved noun readings, preserving alternative senses. No USAS alternatives are cross-joined onto every FrameNet LU.

The head-record zeros are a stated GSE convention, and their presence alone does not satisfy F148's V/U/W test. Cross-bank content is not a quota either: a prime with one positive component is acceptable when its complete lexical-unit profile, including its reference and its combinatorial behavior, is justified (F159). Deictic primes bind their referent in context as construction data: I the speaker, NOW the time of speaking, HERE the speaker's place. That binding writes no cell, so I's tie to the speaker puts no SAY into I, and NOW and HERE carry no WHEN or WHERE (F159).

The hand-authored prime record has a versioned `prime_id`, exponent/lexical-unit identity, GSE reading ID, canonical coordinate and sign, alias mapping if any, typed role and argument slots, documented valencies and combinations, **explicitly warranted** exclusions, source locations, and positive/MUST-NOT readings. A permitted combination stays a permission; only an established exclusion removes candidates. A different sense of the same spelling gets a different GSE reading ID and does not inherit the prime's profile. The atomic record is the lexical axiom; a compiled general bank law is a separately reviewed construction rule. Neither is a dictionary synset chosen by default.

## 3. The state being computed

The unit is a reading key `(surface, lemma, POS, GSE reading ID)` with zero or more **proposed or certified** source-sense links. A WordNet synset or any other source ID is evidence to align, not the identity of the GSE reading. Typed lexical roles state what the word itself denotes or predicates, what it modifies, and which people/things/events are its arguments or members. The roles prevent a property of a participant, instrument, target, or referent from becoming a property of the word's own definition.

For each canonical coordinate, a *possible completed definition* has one state:

| State | Meaning in a completed candidate |
|---|---|
| `+1` | The prime content or positive pole is definitionally present. |
| `−1` | The negative pole is definitionally present; legal only on a bipolar coordinate. |
| `0` | For an unpaired coordinate, the content is explicitly absent in an applicable domain (F118). For a bipolar coordinate, neither pole is present within the applicable domain, including a reviewed middle where meaningful. It is not a missing-information default. |
| `∅` | The coordinate is definitionally silent; no HDC row is written. This requires a coverage or type proof. |

`UNRESOLVED` is the output when surviving candidates disagree about that state; `CONFLICT` means no candidate satisfies admitted hard facts and laws. Neither is a value in a completed candidate. An unpaired coordinate permits `0/+1` when written and may be silent; a bipolar coordinate permits `−1/0/+1` when written and may be silent. **F118 settles unpaired zero as explicit absence within an applicable domain; emptiness is definitional silence.** A rule establishing explicit absence therefore writes zero directly. No source absence, low score or unresolved choice supplies that assertion. A silent row enters neither an HDC dot product nor a norm; comparisons must also report shared written coverage. The existing dense `primitive_values` table cannot represent this artifact faithfully without a mask-aware migration. (F040, F059–F060, F118)

**Serialized contract (F158 §2).** Written `0` is a present row with value 0. Silence has a false written mask and no numeric value. An unresolved cell keeps its remaining domain, null written mask and no value; a coordinate conflict keeps an empty domain. `artifact.validate_sparse`/`read_sparse` refuse inconsistent states, domains, masks and values with named codes. A source-scoped event/role collision can also produce `SOURCE_CONFLICT` without emptying a global coordinate domain; that status remains part of the record and is quarantined by publication and coverage consumers. Every reading is retained in `sparse.jsonl`; only fully settled exportable candidates enter `vectors.jsonl`. Construction homes remain separate in `structures.jsonl`, with explicit `CONSTRUCTION_ONLY` or warranted partial lexical accounts and recorded basis gaps.

An internal candidate may hold multiple slots and relations before projection to the one vector. A signed property of an argument is retained in its role; it is not projected to the target word automatically. If distinct own-content slots demand incompatible signs on one coordinate and the fixed basis cannot represent both, report a conflict or a basis limitation with the roles intact. Do not cancel them to zero.

### Scale is a separate operation on an established vector

A completed lexical artifact is a **bundle** `(masked signed prime vector, relative scale specification, derivation)`. Let `v_i` be a coordinate's state above and let `S` act only on selected, already written, nonzero coordinates. Its realized gain on such a coordinate is positive: `realized_i = gain_i(S) × v_i`, with `gain_i(S) > 0`. This equation describes the required invariants, not a chosen numeric calibration. `S` cannot create a row, delete one, turn `0` into a sign, convert a sign to `0`, or flip a sign. Written zero remains written zero; silence remains silent; an unresolved cell remains unresolved. The scalar's own record is an operator/scale field, not a `NUMBER:+1` row or a 59th coordinate. A scale value can be ordinal and relative without pretending that natural-language intensity has a universal metric.

**INTENSITY is applicability; NUMBER is value.** F125–F126 settle this explicitly: `INTENSITY:+1` records that a measure of intensity is relevant to the definition, not that the word is sufficiently strong to count as “very.” `NUMBER` records what that intensity is. Do not ask a model to threshold plain versus amplified words to decide INTENSITY presence. In a source-guided own gradable-property reading, the guarded degree domain warrants that presence symbolically. A relative-strength observation orders or scales the already active content; it does not manufacture a new prime or mask. The literal atomic declaration remains its own explicitly located axiom and does not acquire every fact of a source-derived ordinary reading of the same spelling.

Thus *tired* and *exhausted* can share the same signed lexical pattern yet occupy different positions in an intensity ordering; *many*, *most* and *all* can likewise be distinguished when their located readings truly share a base pattern. A negative-side word and a positive-side word across a neutral pivot require the appropriate **signed base or antonym direction first**; one sign-preserving scale operation cannot cross that pivot. The examples establish the need for scale, not a preapproved assignment of exact base vectors, selected banks, or numeric gains. The rulebook must document those choices for each scale family and its source warrant. An ordinary NSM prime like `MANY` retains its lexical content; `NUMBER` only orders or magnifies compatible content. The [USAS guide](https://ucrel.lancs.ac.uk/usas/usas_guide.pdf), introduction and “Other symbols utilised,” describes signed positions on semantic scales and doubled/tripled markers for comparative/superlative positions. Preserve that ordinal evidence in the scale channel; do not blindly turn marker count into VERY, HIER_PLUS or another lexical row. (F083, F095–F098, F125–F126)

The builder therefore resolves **two linked questions**: which masked signs are definitionally warranted, and whether typed sources establish a relative scale relation among readings with compatible signs. It must never use a scale observation to manufacture lexical content. A missing scale relation is `UNRESOLVED_SCALE`, not an invented default intensity. For candidate synonyms that differ solely by scale, the same 58-state projection and distinct scale records are a correct outcome. For a claimed scale family whose members need different signs or masks, the family hypothesis is rejected or split; the vector is not coerced to make them match.

## 4. The bank-law registry comes first

Each rule is a versioned, reviewable relation over **allowed combinations**, not an instruction to cast a source vote. Its record contains:

`rule_id · source-of-law · scope/role · antecedent fields and their units · same-reading/link guard · allowed/forbidden tuples · exact consequence · whether absence is closed-world · source lineage · positive witness · MUST-NOT witness · review state`.

For example, the founder's *box* versus *big* distinction (F057, F088) yields a law about an independently denoting slot. An exclusively modifying property reading of *big* explicitly lacks independent objectness; *box* as an object-denoting noun has it. The law must be phrased at the located reading and slot. With F118's state contract, that explicit structural absence writes `SOMETHING:0`. It cannot say that every spelling tagged ADJ is forever non-substantive, nor infer that every other coordinate is silent. POS is one input to the applicability guard. A substantivized adjective is a MUST-NOT witness for over-broad use.

The first registry must distinguish these operations:

| Operation | What a valid instance can do | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Opposite-pole identity | Canonicalize two names to one signed coordinate and shared write state. | Count an antonym as independent evidence. |
| Role/type exclusion | Remove an impossible state from one slot when that slot's kind is proved. | Erase other senses or slots of the spelling. |
| Dependency or required companion | Restrict a second bank when the first content requires its typed argument, bearer, or relation. | Copy an argument's sign onto the word. |
| Permitted combination | Keep a combination such as degree plus evaluation or quantity plus a substantive available. | Assert that every permitted component is present in a particular word. |
| Joint, guarded rule | Constrain two or more components of the same located reading under a proved role relation; remove configurations neither fact removes alone. The components may occupy distinct but linked roles. | Claim “conjunction” merely because two independent filters were applied, or join unrelated senses because their spellings match. |
| Exhaustive obligation | Force a remaining realization when a named definitional component must be represented and all alternatives but one are impossible. | Treat an open dictionary, missing source, or blank field as an exhaustive inventory. |
| Source-link censor | Reject an incompatible POS, sense, argument orientation, or source record. | Turn a rejected candidate into the opposite prime sign. |
| Relative scale relation | Order compatible readings on selected already active coordinates, preserving mask and signs. | Create a lexical prime, cross polarity, scale a silent/zero row into content, or infer intensity from an unrelated word association. |

The rule engine begins with a **joint set of legal configurations** over reading links, roles, definitional obligations, realizations and coordinate states. Write that set as `Ω`. Each admitted, scoped fact and each reviewed law keeps only configurations satisfying it: `Ω′ = {ω ∈ Ω : fact(ω) ∧ law(ω)}`. A coordinate is forced to `v` precisely when **every** survivor has state `v`; a single cell's remaining local options cannot show that on their own when cells are coupled. This is the Sudoku operation in F073–F074: a value can be forced by an exhaustive obligation and eliminations in other slots even though no source directly wrote it. Changes can activate further laws, so propagation repeats to a fixed point. A rule may involve one bank, several banks, or a role relation across banks. The engine need not enumerate all `3^58` or `4^58` products; a factorized constraint representation can retain only active variables and exact relations. Products of separate cell-domain sizes are merely upper bounds on the real joint feasible set. (F058, F067, F069, F073–F076)

For every proposed law, print exactly which assignments it removes, with its guard and counterexample. If the guard is not proved for the target reading, it removes **nothing**. If a source is absent without a closed-world contract, it removes **nothing**. If all configurations disappear, emit `CONFLICT` and identify the incompatible premises rather than projecting a zero vector. No source's popularity or large numerical score can override a hard contradiction.

## 5. How research becomes a rule, rather than an appeal to authority

NSM supplies more than a list of base words. The [official English chart](https://nsm-approach.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Chart-of-NSM-Semantic-Primes_English_v20_May-2022.pdf) gives examples of combinatorial syntax and explicit valency boundaries; [Goddard and Wierzbicka's account of primes and syntax](https://nsm-approach.net/archives/1522) treats combinatorial properties as part of the metalanguage. [Goddard's primes/molecules/templates paper](https://doi.org/10.1515/ling-2012-0022) supplies the larger lexical-typology method. An [author paper archived at ANU](https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/server/api/core/bitstreams/8bc1470c-e7b4-41a4-8161-2a10a1ed8b04/content) notes that prime meanings belong to lexical units, not whole lexemes, and that exponents can have variants. This directly supports sense-specific atomic records and research into legal prime combinations. It does **not** prove that GSE's eight banks, opposite-pole pairing, or every proposed cross-bank exclusion is NSM's own theory.

**What a rule answers to: consistency, not an outside standard of truth (F144).** GSE makes language computable, and what it computes is GSE's own choice. A prime is "correct" because it maps to our intuitive understanding of what words mean, not because it matches an objective truth that no source can supply. The NSM literature is the foundation, and it is read as one aligned, non-contradictory body: when two published analyses of the same word or template component conflict, the later one wins (F137). Beyond the literature lies a hard tail that maps to no unambiguous, objective truth. There the rulebook makes one choice, records its warrant, and applies it everywhere: "Consistency is the only gaurd that prevents this lack of objective truth from becoming degenerate; if everything is built against a self-consistent baseline, then computation maintains perfect correctness" (F144). F144 frames this as accuracy versus precision. Rohan does not claim that GSE or NSM is perfectly accurate. An imperfect instrument becomes exact when it is not read as an absolute measure but used to take two measurements and find their *difference*, and he holds that sparse Hadamard orthogonality in the HDC banks, with vector and matrix operations, makes GSE's precision perfect. For the builder this means three things. A rule is judged by its source and by its consistency with every admitted rule, not re-argued against an outside standard. A choice the sources leave open (the CAUSE contract, the felt valence of feeling nouns, each gold-projection convention) is made once and applied to every reading. A failure is repaired by changing the rule and rebuilding what depends on it, never by fitting one word (F124).

Research-to-rule procedure:

1. **Quote or locate the exact research claim** in a pinned primary source: a prime's valency, allowed frame, documented exclusion, semantic molecule, or lexical template. Record the source version and whether the claim concerns a lexical unit, a syntactic frame, a word class, or a whole semantic type. First use a prime's documented combinations to identify the intended primitive sense of its exponent; then ask which properties can safely become reusable bank laws. An example of a permitted phrase establishes *possibility*, not a universal positive assignment; an unlisted combination is not a prohibition unless the source says so.
2. **Translate it to GSE's state language.** Name the target role, source-level guard, exact allowed and forbidden bank states, and mask consequence. Record where GSE goes beyond NSM, such as signed size/evaluation poles or HDC silence. Do not silently equate an NSM phrase with a GSE coordinate.
3. **Find a positive reading and a MUST-NOT reading** in real sources. Test homographs, noun/verb shifts, metaphors, a property of an argument rather than the target, and a permitted co-occurrence. For the founder's noun/sentiment and evaluator/descriptor sketches, use the examples as hypotheses to refine—not a license for a global `NOUN → GOOD:∅` or global `GOOD excludes BIG` law. The official chart permits evaluation of things and degree with evaluation; the slot matters.
4. **Admit the narrowest surviving rule** to the registry, with provenance and review status. Let a rule stay a candidate when the research does not establish an exact consequence. Run it over a stratified lexical sample to find over-generalization, then specialize the guard rather than weakening correctness.
5. **Check the whole closure.** A law valid alone may contradict another law on a compound reading. Test single-prime seeds, pairs, and higher-order combinations, plus real non-atomic readings. Keep counterexamples in the regression corpus.

The same discipline applies to NLP datasets. Their documentation defines what a field encodes and at what granularity. The lexicon rulebook then states which combinations of fields and roles license a GSE state. The research is a source of **typed premises and conditional constraints**, not a precomputed set of word vectors. This is the Harmonizing/Triagegeist lesson Rohan identified: structure first, cheap selection only on the residual. (F014–F019, F057–F059)

### 5.1 Discover the bank relations from the shared English population

F101 and F106–F111 add an empirical route to finding the rules. **Every dataset observes English; the shared word is the join anchor.** Assemble the native columns and categorical assertions for each word, then measure how they constrain one another across the population. Discovery does not wait for a new sense parser. Keep source POS, alternatives, frames, classes, roles and ratings as observed fields: these can explain when a relation holds and supply its guard. A later lexical rule applies at the appropriate reading/role, but that does not prevent word-level discovery now.

Harmonizing supplies the operation: known cell-line facts constrain organism, sex, disease and material type; organism constraints censor inappropriate fields; the ledger retains original evidence while typed consequences accumulate. Its code loads or authors those relations. GSE's new discovery pass must **extract candidate conditional relations from the overlapping lexical datasets**, then validate their semantic consequence using the field contracts and NLP/NSM research. It must not import Harmonizing's domain rules or infer that a high agreement score itself proves a lexical law.

For two source observations A and B, use only words observed in both sources. Count the full contingency table, not just matching examples. Measure the directional rate P(B|A), the target's base rate, the signed coherence `C = 2 P(B|A) − 1`, uncertainty bounds, and the words that contradict the relation. Retain the sign: perfect opposition is a meaningful relation and must not become agreement merely by taking an absolute value. Source absence contributes no observed cell. Explicit native zero remains a native zero; a blank field, NRC association zero, written lexical zero and proven bank silence have different contracts.

The single-bank pass is only the beginning. Test **joint** native conditions A∧B against C on the same three-source word population, comparing the joint result with each premise alone. Do not shortlist combinations solely by strong singleton correlations: *Significance Weighting* §13 identifies complementary bridges, where one premise becomes useful only with another. Report the extra conditional information and counterexamples. Test reproducibility on disjoint word partitions, and inspect POS/category strata and related word families before treating a pattern as a scoped law. Hash splits are a reproducibility check, not proof of universal English behavior or independence between related words.

### 5.2 Rank by what a rule resolves now, and recompute after it acts

The relevant lesson of *Significance Weighting* §§5, 7b, 12–14 is **marginal resolution**, not raw recurrence or chain length. A hundred redundant observations need not resolve more than one decisive conjunction. For an admitted rule r and current rule set P, measure the additional lawful configuration exclusions or resolved states produced by closure under P∪{r}, over the current word population. A possible exact measure is `Σ_w log₂(|Ω_P(w)| / |Ω_(P∪{r})(w)|)` where the feasible sets are actually computable and nonempty. If only newly resolved cells or observed endpoints are counted, call that a **coverage analogue**, not measured entropy or an exact value of κ. Conflicts are reported separately.

Apply a validated relation, propagate its consequences, then recompute the residual and the marginal value of the remaining rules. A bridge can increase another rule's value; a redundant rule can fall to zero. Do not import a static submodular bound, a knee location, or the paper's measured constants into this changing rule graph. Native records must corroborate proposed relations independently of the builder's own derived signs; otherwise the loop would be measuring its own assumptions. Counterexamples can reveal a missing POS, role, source-scope or semantic-type condition: specialize and re-test the rule when the data warrants it.

The first executable discovery pass is [scripts/lexicon_coherence.py](/Users/rohanvinaik/Projects/gse/scripts/lexicon_coherence.py). Its [measured report](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/coherence-2026-10-24/REPORT.md) and complete `results.json` record source hashes, overlaps, continuous relations, held-out directional candidates, joint gains, and counterexamples. It observes native source dimensions; it does not use old generated prime signs as labels or claim to have completed the lexical rulebook. The next construction step is to turn the strongest scoped relations into explicit allowed/forbidden bank tuples, with research warrants and two-sign witnesses, then measure their actual closure contribution.

### 5.3 How the rules were obtained, where they are saved, and how they become executable

This is the persistent chain from raw sources to a lexical assignment:

The coherence run used NFC/casefold/strip word identity, retained source categories and native numeric units, and excluded missing-source observations from each population. Discovery used hash folds 0–2 and held-out checking used folds 3–4. These are **candidate-discovery criteria**, not definitions of truth. Related words can cross a hash split; this is not a family-independent linguistic proof.

That is useful conditional information, with 15 retained counterexamples. It does **not** itself establish `FEEL:+1`, `GOOD:-1`, disgust, or a silent bank. The proposed lexical rule must first explain the word's own definitional role and what those fields actually assert. Conversely, an explicit good/bad-quality category plus a compatible own-quality lexical unit supplies a different sort of premise from a human affect rating.

Admission of an empirically discovered relation must add a rulebook record containing its `COH_…` references, semantic mechanism, exact guard and allowed/forbidden tuples, role and scope, research locations, supporting and MUST-NOT words, exceptions and their remedies, and a review state. Re-run it across the shared population and preserve its failures. Investigating an exception is a bounded typed task for the small model: discriminate a missing condition, wrong scope/alignment, association-only relation, or genuine unresolved case; then test the proposed condition again. After admission, remeasure the rule's marginal closure contribution and its interactions with existing rules.

**Executable admission contract.** `core.rule_admission_reason` reports the precise reason used by `registry.split_by_admission`. A current validated record requires an ACTIVE lifecycle, a FOUNDATIONAL or CONDITIONAL tier, typed authority and source references, a warrant, positive and MUST-NOT test node IDs, and a VALIDATED receipt whose `contract_sha256` matches every rule field except the receipt itself. Changing the guard, consequence, authority or lifecycle invalidates that receipt. Empirical authority requires a conditional tier. A legacy founder approval is admitted without a new receipt only for the exact digest in the recorded legacy-approval table; a new `FOUNDER_APPROVED` string grants no authority. The gate executes the referenced intent tests; schema acceptance alone does not prove their result. Computed admission dispositions belong in the execution receipt, not a hand-maintained count here.

Withheld registry rules write no cells, assert no enabling facts and create no model questions. Their potential writes are retained separately. Source projectors also carry explicit, tested application contracts: native event/role predicates, nominal own-target conventions and hand-authored profiles retain their source identities and code hashes. They are not admitted by an association score. F144's historical decisions remain in [rule_decisions.json](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/closeout-2026-10-25/rule_decisions.json); neither a modified implementation nor this document creates another founder approval.

**The association catalog is diagnostic.** `conditionals.py` validates and compiles the content-addressed native catalog, retaining a named disposition for every candidate. At `WORD_ALTERNATIVE_UNION` scope it can report a met/unmet antecedent, corroborated or contradicted native association, missing observation, unavailable resource or unsupported feature. These are native-source diagnostics, not lexical signs. `may_write_lexical_cells:false` remains binding. A catalog entry may link to a separately admitted conditional lexical rule, but the link does not admit that rule or make a word-level association a same-sense fact. Catalog diagnostics never enter the solver as builder-confirmed premises.

**Exceptions retract an exact application.** A `RuleException` names the reading, rule, independent source record, reason and evidence kind; an application ID is required whenever the rule has multiple applicable contracts. Only conditional applications can be withdrawn. Missing, ambiguous, duplicate, foundational and builder/model-derived corrections receive distinct named refusals. The solver starts again from the remaining observations and laws, so downstream unsupported cells reopen while independent support survives. Retraction is local: it does not confirm, demote or rewrite the global law from the builder's own output. Wider guard refinement or lifecycle changes require another source-backed rule contract and validation receipt.

#### Redundancy check required before claiming a new constraint

F116 asks whether the apparent FrameNet/VerbNet connection adds information or repeats an existing assertion. The [class audit](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/coherence-2026-10-24/EXPERIENCER_OBJ_REDUNDANCY.json) and [same-frame audit](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/coherence-2026-10-24/EXPERIENCER_OBJ_FRAME_AUDIT.json) test `COH_5162873620c04d58`, the 127/127 `Experiencer_obj → cause` relation. **119 of those words belong to one VerbNet class, `amuse-31.1`; all 242 members of that class in this native single-word population inherit a causation predicate from the class's frames.** These are many lexical membership observations, but not 119 independently authored causation predicates. FrameNet's own frame definition already asserts that a stimulus provokes an emotion. For the causal component, the two representations largely repeat one assertion.

The remaining eight words are not evidence of a new cross-bank law either. Checking actual frames found `cause` and `emotional_state` together for 121 of the 127 words. The six without that typed conjunction are *conciliate, destroy, embitter, kill, petrify,* and *rattle*. Their generic VerbNet causation does not establish alignment with FrameNet's emotional reading. For example, the local *kill* frame represents causing death, *destroy* represents destruction, and *rattle* has sound-emission frames. The original word-level agreement remains a real native measurement, but it is insufficient to infer a same-reading causal-emotion correspondence. A missing emotional predicate is not a proof of absent emotion in English; it limits this proposed bridge's warrant.

The [saved review](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/rule-registry-2026-10-24/rule_reviews.jsonl) therefore classifies this candidate as **resource alignment / largely redundant causal content, with unresolved reading scope**, and leaves it unadmitted. Preserve both source observations and their lineage, but canonicalize equivalent claims at the reading/event/role level before computing additional resolution. Preserve class inheritance once at its origin, rather than counting its copies as independent predicate annotations. A compatible second representation can check a reading or supply missing roles without resolving an already fixed coordinate again. Only a genuinely additional condition or relation earns marginal resolution credit.

The [official SemLink repository](https://github.com/cu-clear/semlink) documents mappings between FrameNet, VerbNet and PropBank; the [VerbNet documentation](https://verbs.colorado.edu/verbnet/) describes class-level predicate structures and inheritance. This does not prove that the particular local entries were copied from one another. Direct copying remains unestablished. The historical discovery label `DISTINCT_SOURCE_CHANNELS` means two different resource names, **not verified statistical or annotation independence**. Apply this dependency and semantic-subsumption check to other candidates before admission. Redundancy, a genuine cross-bank constraint, an associative relation, and a false reading join require different remedies.

### 5.4 ModelAtlas principles for the uncertain remainder

F119 explicitly assigns the hard tail to the small model; F121 points to ModelAtlas as an additional idea source. Its [architecture upgrade](/Users/rohanvinaik/tools/ModelAtlas/docs/architecture-upgrade.md), [typed emission contract](/Users/rohanvinaik/tools/ModelAtlas/src/model_atlas/contract.py), [certifier](/Users/rohanvinaik/tools/ModelAtlas/src/model_atlas/certifier/certifier.py), [rule library](/Users/rohanvinaik/tools/ModelAtlas/src/model_atlas/certifier/rules.py), and [per-bank coherence implementation](/Users/rohanvinaik/tools/ModelAtlas/src/model_atlas/bank_coherence.py) were read as complete units on 2026-10-25. They supply the following construction principles, adapted to GSE's definition contract:

1. **Keep each observation before its projection.** A derived sign retains the original field, value, scope, record ID and derivation. Several copies of one inherited annotation do not become several independent observations. The candidate vector cannot corroborate itself.
2. **Separate three kinds of work.** A proved structural consequence is assigned or excluded symbolically. A bounded unresolved choice is sent to the small model. An unavailable source or unimplemented field contract is recorded as such; the model must not fabricate a source assertion to conceal it. A rule needs to be correct wherever its guard holds; it need not cover every word before the model can be used.
3. **Make the residual an explicit contract.** `plan-tail` considers only projector-nominated residual coordinates with established source record IDs. A work item names the reading, coordinate, exact surviving options, independent premises and diagnosed reason. Unavailable evidence or an unimplemented projection is not permission to nominate every open coordinate. The routine payload remains typed source observations, never an unrestricted gloss or reference answer. The queue has explicit total/per-reading limits; actual execution also has a call budget and a task ledger.
4. **Select only among actual surviving options.** ModelAtlas's bank-scoped vocabulary and refinement loop are useful precedents: never ask about an already uniform dimension or offer an option excluded by the current constraints. GSE's selection schema is generated from the surviving domain or coupled choices. The model neither replaces fixed signs nor invents a coordinate, label or numeric confidence as authority.
5. **Check additions and omissions, then propagate.** ModelAtlas's `requires`/`forbids` distinction is the important two-sided operation. In GSE, reject a proposed fill if it contradicts admitted hard constraints; add consequences required by the combined facts, and repeat closure before choosing the next decision. Preserve candidate/model provenance even after propagation. Schema validity establishes format; consistency establishes compatibility; neither alone establishes lexical correctness. F049 permits provisional closure after a bounded omission challenge; F118 determines whether the resulting state is explicit zero or silence.
6. **Route disagreement about the same claim.** ModelAtlas's usable coherence example compares independently sourced estimates of one bank quantity. Different orthogonal banks need not agree with one another. GSE's cross-bank relations instead test whether their joint configuration satisfies the warranted conditional law. A coherence score can locate disagreement or prioritize investigation; it cannot average incompatible signs into zero, vote a mask into existence, or assign a lexical sign by itself.

This is a transfer of principles, not a dependency on ModelAtlas or a literal copy of its heuristics. Its invocation thresholds, trust tiers and geometry are not GSE truth rules or completeness tests. GSE repeatedly closes its own constraints. A diagnosed residual model choice is permitted after the symbolic foundation; ordinary builds do not instantiate a model. The CLI requires an explicit tail queue, binds each task to the reading's actual premises and exact current domain, and records selection, abstention, stale task, already-resolved task, budget exhaustion or backend failure separately. Every surviving lexical state, including `0`, remains selectable; an emotion-specific signed-or-open constraint excludes `0` only where that sourced rule applies.

The end-to-end obligation is therefore: `native observations → applicable constraints → closure → bounded typed choice → checked candidate constraint → closure → recomputed residual → addition/omission challenge → masked candidate artifact`. A small, legal model decision that produces no new coordinate may still reject an unsupported channel; the receipt must say whether it changed the feasible set. It must not be advertised as completing a vector merely because a model returned valid JSON.

## 7. Construction path for one word, then every word

1. **Freeze the contract.** Pin the 72-name/58-coordinate basis and aliases, separate NUMBER operation, rulebook and authored foundation, source files, declared worklist, implementation hashes and artifact schema. Preserve source alternatives and licenses. A full sweep means every declared input has an outcome, including no applicable rule; it does not mean every English sense has a complete definition.
2. **Open located readings.** Join surface observations without collapsing their scope. Keep adjective source alternatives, native verb frames, noun LUs, prime homographs and function-word constructions separate. Source absence, observed zero, unsupported fields and incompatible joins remain different observations.
3. **Construct through one path.** Each family emits a `ReadingSeed` containing typed observations, guarded laws, source/application identities and its output layout. `reading.construct_reading` uses the same `core.resolve` fixed-point and joint-support solver for profiles, adjectives, verbs and nouns. A source adapter does not write prime signs.
4. **Retain exact proof and corrections.** A forced state retains its source and rule trace. Prime decisions additionally require independently rooted attribution (§2). An exact conditional exception retracts only its named application and triggers resolution from remaining premises; a contradiction is never averaged into zero.
5. **Emit the symbolic result.** Settled signs, explicit zeros, authored/type-warranted silence, remaining domains and conflicts are serialized separately. Unsupported cells stay open. A source-scoped collision remains quarantined even if other global cell domains are nonempty. No ordinary reading inherits a prime's authored boundary.
6. **Optionally execute diagnosed tail work.** The ordinary build has no selector. An explicit source-bound task queue may request only nominated unresolved components and the exact surviving states, including legal `0`, plus an explicit abstention. Execution checks the task domain immediately before each decision, enforces the call budget, adds a model-candidate restriction, closes again and recomputes. Gold cannot choose tasks, fields, options or payloads.
7. **Evaluate and publish with their limits.** Independent reference comparison reports additions, omissions, wrong poles, zero/silence disagreement, unresolved components and source conflict. The corpus audit measures surface homing and evidence separately from coordinate completion. SQLite publication preserves these states and provenance; it does not certify lexical truth or choose a sense for the user.

The batch repeats this same reading path over the frozen worklist. A rule, profile or source revision invalidates dependent artifacts and requires a rebuild; old and new closures are not silently mixed.

Each corpus metric has its own named 90% threshold result: declared vocabulary, a lexical/structural home, signed evidence, head-zero/other-zero evidence and complete coordinate profiles. A partial head-only or head-zero-only reading is not a completed definition. A SOURCE_CONFLICT reading contributes diagnostic counts but no signed/zero support or coordinate-completion coverage. Neither a high homing percentage nor fully settled coordinates establishes semantic completeness or production readiness.

Known source-coverage residuals remain explicit: unmapped native predicates and nominal target frames; unresolved event/role or same-sense alignment; abstract, temporal, locative and untyped collective noun readings without a reviewed head contract; construction-level basis gaps; uncovered source families from §6; unhandled morphological/lexical alternatives; and scale families without calibrated gains. A model cannot fabricate the missing source assertion. A diagnosed bounded task can select among established legal alternatives; otherwise the reading stays partial.

## 9. Decisions the rulebook must not fake

- **Unpaired zero — resolved:** F040 specifies `0/+1` as legal written values; F118 defines `0` as explicit absence within an applicable domain and empty as definitional silence. Apply this contract; do not reopen it as an unspecified design decision or fill unexamined cells with zero.
- **Noun sentiment and adjective objectness:** F057 supplies powerful examples of own-role versus associated meaning, and F088 makes bank relations the base operation. Those examples are a research and counterexample program, not permission to silently impose a global POS-to-sign table. Mark any broader law `CANDIDATE` until it survives contrary lexical readings. **Feeling nouns — resolved (F144):** a noun whose own denotatum is a feeling carries that feeling's felt valence in GOOD, as the NSM explications of feeling nouns state it ("X felt something bad"): −1 for the Fear nouns, the certified pole for a valence-scale tag (so *hatred* is −1), and a typed field for nouns tagged only E5− or E6− outside Fear. That is the experiencer's felt quality, not a value judgement of a referent. F057's core stands for every other noun: *Satan* and *dog* get no GOOD sign from reputation or association.
- **CAUSE on BECAUSE — resolved (F159):** the connective does not itself denote the causative event, so under option B the prime BECAUSE leaves CAUSE silent.
- **What correctness answers to — resolved (F144):** consistency with the sourced, recorded baseline, not an outside standard of truth (§5). A choice the sources leave open is made once, with its warrant, and applied to every reading.
- **Research versus adaptation:** the NSM prime inventory, grammar and molecules are research sources. GSE's 72-name basis, 14 signed aliases, eight storage banks, ternary signs, masks and HDC comparison are local choices. Record mappings and deviations explicitly.
- **Scale versus lexical content:** `NUMBER` is Rohan's hierarchical/combinatorial extension, not an NSM prime, GSE's 73rd prime, or another semantic bank. The exact numeric gain, its calibration, and which bank components a scale family selects require a reviewed rule and source alignment. A count of 73 computational registry entries must not overwrite the 72 lexical-unit inventory.
- **Degree relevance versus degree value — resolved:** F125–F126 define INTENSITY as the presence of a relevant measure; NUMBER encodes its value. A model must not decide INTENSITY presence by classifying a word as ordinary versus VERY-like.
- **Repairs — shared symbolic law:** F124 forbids fitting the system to an individual failing word. Amend the general assignment relation or apply an explicit post-assignment rule, then retain the failing witness and contrary readings as tests.
- **Completeness:** no ordinary open-world source alone proves that all definitional obligations are listed. The omission challenge must identify a bounded unit and its coverage basis; otherwise output `PARTIAL` rather than a deceptively sparse “complete” vector.
- **Model authority:** a tiny residual is an empirical goal, not a promise. A model selection may finish a candidate record but cannot make an invalid source link or law true. Promotion criteria need measured error classes and review of the Zipf cohort.
- **Definition versus interpretation:** richer lexical encodings can improve vector identification and simultaneously widen downstream inference. Do not score GSE-01 by one “semantic clarity” number or feed world affect back as lexical GOOD/BAD.
