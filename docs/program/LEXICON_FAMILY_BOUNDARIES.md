# Ordinary-family definition boundaries

This extends the active construction design under Rohan's instruction to proceed with the family milestone. The fixed basis remains 72 lexical primes over 58 signed coordinates plus the separate NUMBER operation. F098 states NUMBER's operational contract; F148 also calls it a special meta-prime and, in an aside, a 73rd prime. The terminology differs; neither instruction requires a new lexical coordinate or a change in sign/mask behavior.

## Contract and authority

An ordinary-family boundary is an authored, conditional GSE projection convention. It is not inferred from an absent dictionary entry or from all inspected explications omitting a prime. Primary NSM analyses may inform a reusable construction template; those examples then become development evidence. Evaluation answers never enter runtime construction or task selection.

Each of the 58 coordinates has one authored account in the admitted source rule's `definition_boundary`:

| Account | Construction effect | Completion obligation |
|---|---|---|
| REQUIRED | No write. Independent source and bank laws must derive its singleton state. | A missing warrant leaves it unresolved; a wrong forced value refuses family completion. Written zero is permitted only where the scoped head/absence rule establishes it. |
| OPTIONAL | Retain the legal domain; nominate a model task only under a separately reviewed task-type policy. | Resolve symbolically or through an admitted typed selection; withheld tasks and abstention preserve the domain. |
| SILENT | A guarded, individually reasoned conditional silence law. | Retain the authored warrant and application identity. Its retraction reopens the coordinate. |

An unaccounted coordinate makes the contract invalid. A required coordinate is never silently moved into the boundary when its content warrant is removed. A complete coordinate assignment and an independently established correct lexical definition remain different claims. Candidates retain their provisional status.

## First family: adjectival Fear experiencer states

`GSE_FEAR_ADJ_BOUNDARY_V1` applies only through admitted `EMOTION_FEAR_ADJ`: a located ADJ E5-/E6- reading joined to FrameNet Fear. The source adapter excludes slash tags, positive poles and incompatible POS/frame readings. The boundary additionally requires the source-family and modifier-role observations. Feeling nouns, causative verbs and neighboring emotional/personality frames inherit no boundary.

The machine-readable rule records every coordinate's reason and source references. Independent rules must supply the 20 required states. Six fields remain unresolved without additional construction support: DO, IF, KNOW, THEN, MAYBE and MUST. Every rule-surviving value, including zero, remains legal. MUST is outside this class's model queue and stays open; CAN and NOT alone do not establish its nested practical-necessity scope. The other 32 coordinates have individually authored silence accounts. Revision 4 below governs the scoped MAYBE domain and task-type review. These boundaries are tested hypotheses about this selected projection, not universal claims about all fear-related words.

The existing Fear-aversion relation supplies the genuine coupled witness: FEEL and THINK together force WANT and NOT within its sourced scope. No relation between DO, IF and KNOW is invented for the demonstration. Removing FEEL's independent warrant must reopen FEEL and the aversion consequences rather than using the expected profile to reconstruct them.

Revision 2 narrows the optional temporal slot to NOW or an unassigned reference. Future `will` in an anticipated event does not supply positive THEN under the existing GSE explication projection. This is a shared authored template restriction supported by the development analyses, not a word-specific correction or a newly held-out result. A definition that requires an explicit non-now anchor needs a separate scoped refinement. Selector payloads carry the signed basis vocabulary (including THEN/NOW) and the source rule's feeling-plus-prototypical-thought scope on both library and explicit-queue routes.

Revision 3 adds the independently typed SOMETIMES typicality claim and its shared quantified-time decomposition law. QUANTITY and WHEN follow from that claim, so QUANTITY no longer consumes a model choice. This corrects an unnecessary open degree of freedom exposed by the retrospective omission checks. The chart's portmanteau account and the development templates supply the warrant; model output does not establish the rule. Withholding the decomposition rule reopens QUANTITY while independent WHEN support remains.

## Revision 4: informative typed selection

Rohan's 2026-10-26 correction governs the earlier experiment described above. F047, F066 and F078 permit a model to select among surviving states using typed context including the word. Sources need not determine its answer first. Evaluation must show that each task type beats always-yes, always-silent and class-majority selectors before its answers are used in construction. Reference answers, scores and majority tables stay in evaluation; construction consumes an explicit reviewed task-type policy with scope, status and evidence references.

`FEAR_OPTIONAL_COMPONENT_V1` is WITHHELD after the uninformative development result. Both library and explicit queue enforce this status. The planner emits no tasks for it; an old queue or bank-fill route cannot bypass it. All six optional fields remain unresolved. MUST is outside this class's queue, without asserting silence or zero. Future admitted task types may still resolve uncertain fields and propagate consequences; mechanism-only test admissions confer no lexical approval.

The scoped MAYBE account allows present or unaddressed epistemic possibility. Its applicable explicit absence would claim absence of uncertainty; the separately scoped noun dread's subjective-certainty analysis supplies no such warrant for the selected adjectives. Revision 4 therefore excludes MAYBE 0 only in this family, under Rohan's stated rule. DO, IF, KNOW, THEN and MUST retain their legal zero. There is no general zero-warrant gate.

The old five-complete-candidate headline is withdrawn as semantic completion: its optional selections did not carry demonstrated information. The new diagnosis packet preserves those trials, builds a symbolic ledger over all 13 references, and reports random-word resolution without gold or model calls. Disagreements diagnose shared rules, independently evidenced exceptions or open cells; they are not word-specific tuning targets.

No untouched published positive Fear reference was found in that search. Report those classes separately. ## Engineering path and obligations

CLI build → admitted source match → `adjective_seed` → `families.family_laws` → ordinary `construct_reading`/`core.resolve` → optional diagnosed queue with propagation after each answer → `account_family` export check → sparse/candidate artifacts → existing SQLite publisher and reader.

`families.py` owns contract validation, guarded boundary compilation and non-writing obligation checks. The enclosing source rule owns authority, lifecycle, digest and positive/must-not admission witnesses. The shared constructor owns the completion check; both CLI tail and library construction consume it. Artifact serialization retains the check result. No second closure engine or language parser is added.

Required witnesses cover complete ordinary candidate export, malformed/incomplete contracts, source-scope refusal, withheld-rule refusal, deletion of independent content support, silence ablation, explicit-zero retention, abstention, and real entry-point execution. Semantic differences must be replayed before any frozen baseline update. Source, model and evaluation identities remain separate.

This milestone changes construction behavior deliberately. It claims no speedup. Its acceptance packet must state which outputs are complete candidates, which reference disagreements remain, and which independent evaluation classes were available.
