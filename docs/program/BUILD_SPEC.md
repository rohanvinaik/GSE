Status: design contract, not a claim of present implementation. Read [START_HERE.md](START_HERE.md), [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md), the pinned source manifest, and the actual producer/consumer code before taking a package. Stable IDs below appear in `WORK_INDEX.json`. Completion requires positive and MUST-NOT witnesses, a real-entry-point receipt, and a per-class outcome ledger.

<a id="gse-00"></a>
## GSE-00 — establish the live contract

Trace the text entry points through parser, NSM/HDC encoder, role/operator derivation, document context, repair, profile, Sparse Wiki consumer, and Regenesis exporter. Distinguish imported, tested-only, and production-called paths. Record artifact schema/version, state codes, duplicate implementations, data digests, and baseline results. Reproduce dated proof-packet demonstrations or mark them unverified; do not infer completion from plans. The output is a current capability map and fixtures that protect existing correct readings. MUST-NOT: a defined or passing test-only symbol is reported as production capacity.

<a id="gse-01"></a>
## GSE-01 — evidence-backed lexicon quality pipeline

The lexical artifact remains definitional: fixed-version NSM prime basis, ternary sign, sense/POS and derivation evidence. Inventory the live lexicon and its source lineage before regeneration. The **build** covers the complete declared worklist and every located lexical sense in scope; frequency does not remove rare words or senses from that worklist. A worklist must state its boundary and unresolved source-absent or nonlexical entries; it cannot claim to contain every possible English word. Gather licensed primary lexical/reference material and user-authored definitions; use cheap agents for bounded configuration selection, contradiction discovery, omission challenges, and triage. Agents return only surviving typed choices, cited source records, and uncertainty; deterministic validators project the resulting signs and enforce schema, closed basis, configuration constraints, duplicate/sense collisions, and provenance. Human or explicitly authorized canonicalization promotes candidates. DeepNSM is silver anchor/evaluation material only: LLM-generated, CC-BY-NC-SA, never shipped as lexicon content. Keep frequency/coverage, source/license ledger, abstention and per-class error samples. Do not import encyclopedic facts or world affect into lexical signs.

**Construction order:** establish the bank-relation laws and hand-defined prime **lexical-unit records** first. Each prime record includes the combinatorial profile that distinguishes its primitive sense from other senses of the same exponent; reviewed properties can become reusable bank laws only with their valid guards and scope. The 72 lexical primes project through 14 antonym pairs to 58 signed coordinates. `NUMBER` is a separate scale operation on selected already active coordinates, never a 73rd lexical prime: it preserves mask, zero and sign while retaining relative intensity among otherwise identical vectors. Typed source observations and coupled source-reading/type/role/scope conditions then narrow the **feasible joint bank configurations** to a fixed point before a model is called. A small model chooses only among the remaining typed configurations; a reviewed projector derives signs and silence states and a separately warranted scale relation. Preserve the conditional-collapse trace, source dependencies, and contradictions. The active contract is the [lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md), grounded in [Rohan's founder words](LEXICON_FOUNDER_WORDS_2026-10-24.md). The deleted factor compiler and experimental gloss/clause and all-prime paths are not the implementation to revive.

**Correctness includes completeness.** An extra, false sign and an omitted, warranted sign are both wrong. A sparse vector is correct only if its sparsity survives an explicit search for missing definitional components. Keep the ternary vector and its write mask separate from an epistemic state for each canonical prime: written positive, written negative where the prime is bipolar, written middle `0`, proven silent `∅`, unresolved, or conflict. The 14 mapped opposite poles inherit the corresponding reviewed decision. An unexamined or unresolved component must not silently become `0` or proven silence. A source quotation locates a premise; it does not by itself prove semantic entailment. Trace any definitional unpacking to the fixed basis, with the source, sense and derivation chain visible; refuse unsupported or circular expansion. Candidate vectors with incomplete coverage remain partial and cannot be promoted as a complete replacement.

**Validation is Zipfian.** Derive a pinned surface-form cohort that accounts for at least 90% of eligible token mass in a broad frequency source and at least 90% of eligible tokens in an independent real-text corpus; check its genre slices separately. Deeply audit every cohort form through the actual GSE entry point, including closed-class/operator routing, morphology, attested POS, competing senses, source-to-sign warrants, **missing-sign challenges**, and negative witnesses. Exact absence from a lexeme table is not a failure verdict when the form has a structural home. Outside the cohort, retain deterministic validation for every built case and stratified adversarial audits across POS, prime bank, polysemy, source availability and the frequency tail. Report false additions and false omissions separately, alongside identified, abstained, wrong, unavailable and partial by class and by measured token mass; never promote a whole corpus from one aggregate score. Acceptance: replayed full-worklist build with hashes, this measured two-sided audit, adversarial polysemy/function-word/negation tests, and unchanged previously correct entries unless reviewed. MUST-NOT: dog/cat proximity is treated as a lexicon defect merely because referents differ.

<a id="gse-02"></a>
## GSE-02 — consume Sparse Wiki through the existing grounding path

Specify a versioned world-provider result at the existing `grounding`/genealogy resolution seam: candidates, typed IDs, source/data digest, signed bank and hierarchy path, direction and hop provenance, missing/partial/ambiguous status. Keep GSE's `_select_by_coherence` for linguistic/structural sense choice on discrete type threads and the guarded secondary HDC rerank; Sparse Wiki owns world-referent selection and its derivation path. No cosine world resolver or flat word-to-prime lookup. Move source world ingestion, world facts, world EPA, aliases, and traversal implementation to Sparse Wiki. Current `scripts/build_genealogy_db.py` world tables are a transitional derived cache until a parallel real-entry comparison permits retirement. Check exact-name versus alias collision: current bare `Jordan` selects only Country in the GSE derivative while standalone search surfaces person/river candidates. Acceptance: country and Michael Jordan remain candidates in relevant mention/context, with distinct world paths and no silently vanished candidate. MUST-NOT: a missing canonical Sparse Wiki DB yields a successful word-only world build or is mislabeled as no world referent.

<a id="gse-03"></a>
## GSE-03 — SymbolicSpellCheck and controlled repair

Make correctness a typed question: valid token, valid name/referent, grammatical fit, intended contextual use, repair candidate, committed repair, or abstention. The healer uses one statement as intervention scope and bounded nearby context (roughly ±2 lines) as signal scope; coding-sequence and regulatory repairs keep separate traces. Preserve correct names and values. A correction cannot be committed solely because it produces a more common sentence; source text and alternatives remain recoverable. Acceptance: real text entry handles misspelling, valid unusual word, named entity, quoted error, and structurally misread sentence. MUST-NOT: spellcheck changes an already correct token without a stronger convergent warrant.

<a id="gse-04"></a>
## GSE-04 — genre, register, and prose-style observations

Audit `gse/discourse/profile.py`, `nested_router.py`, `infer.py`, trained artifacts, training/evaluation data and actual callers before describing the abandoned genre-profiler work. Extend deterministic document measurements of operators, complexity, coherence, affect, register and style with support counts, spans, provenance and abstention. Genre is finer than register; style is a realized feature profile. Any trained tail is bounded to a typed inference over these measurements, versioned and compared with structural evidence; it cannot replace it or decide narrative truth. Acceptance: per-class identified/abstained/wrong ledgers across varied genres, short/empty/mixed documents, and distribution shift. MUST-NOT: an unavailable model artifact silently changes deterministic profile semantics.

<a id="gse-05"></a>
## GSE-05 — multiscale composition and Janus inputs

Compose word, sentence, nearby context and document scale once. Cassettes resolve one in-frame uncertainty using out-of-frame evidence; each reports support/convergence and preserves a correct value. Publish validated context immediately for dependent decisions. Produce literal proposition/roles, affect, genre/style observations, and source spans usable by the Janus expected-pattern comparison in Regenesis. GSE does not make irony or causal-story conclusions here. Acceptance: a contextual repair works through the real document entry while an already correct isolated sentence survives; data lineage prevents correlated measurements from masquerading as separate warrants. MUST-NOT: an empty document or failed cassette fabricates a confident interpretation.

<a id="gse-06"></a>
## GSE-06 — versioned computable-language export

Define a serialization contract for sentence/document structure, prime-basis version, exact vectors/signs, roles, operators/modality/negation, repair ledger, profile observations, world candidate references, spans, confidence basis and named partial/abstention codes. Preserve source text and transformation provenance. Exercise the actual Regenesis consumer, not just local schema validation. Coordinate field names and absence states with Regenesis RG-01; pin only verified compatible commit/data combinations in the Regenesis integration lock. Acceptance: round-trip representative and adversarial inputs without changing categorical codes, dtype, shape, zero/fallback meaning or evidence lineage. MUST-NOT: absent world grounding is serialized as an empty confirmed world.

<a id="gse-07"></a>
## GSE-07 — assurance, performance and repeatability

Implement the shared [ASSURANCE.md](ASSURANCE.md) contract in this repo after entry points are stable. Record calls from the real CLI, replay data-free costly kernels with exact strides/dtypes/writeability and input immutability; pair whole-entry timing against an unchanged baseline. Run Detective with intent and negative specification, plus symmetric local/remote gates. Acceptance is a reproducible receipt tied to source/data/lock hashes and a per-class error ledger. MUST-NOT: a fast kernel result alone is reported as end-to-end acceleration.

## Cross-repository exit

GSE's exit artifact is computable, traceable English with independent world references and bounded profile observations. A Regenesis real-entry corpus must consume it without hidden GSE world reconstruction. World identity/path correctness is signed off against Sparse Wiki SW-04/SW-05, and reasoning correctness against Regenesis RG-03/RG-06.
