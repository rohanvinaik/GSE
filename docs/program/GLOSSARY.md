# GSE glossary — theoretical commitments at their code locations

Read the linked whole source units before treating a term as settled. Entries distinguish the program's intended meaning from a historical implementation. The adjacent [Sparse Wiki](../../../sparse-wiki-grounding/docs/program/GLOSSARY.md) and [Regenesis](../../../Regenesis/docs/program/GLOSSARY.md) glossaries own their channels.

| Term | Meaning and boundary | Current place and deeper reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Meaning vs. lexical definition** | Meaning combines the word's definition with grammar, context, world grounding, and later inference. The lexicon encodes only the narrow definitional contribution over a fixed basis. It must assert every warranted lexical sign and no unwarranted one; it is neither a complete account of meaning nor an NSM explication. A more fully identified definition can support more downstream inference paths. | [Lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md); `gse/primitives_v2.py`, `gse/curation/`. |
| **Definitional lexicon** | A located reading's lexical contribution is a masked balanced-ternary sign over the build's fixed prime basis. Bank relations determine legal combinations and a few typed choices may settle many states at once. Encyclopedic facts, referent identity, and world affect are Sparse Wiki's channel. Near-identical signatures for `dog` and `cat` can be correct; role and referent identity keep propositions distinct. | [Lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md); `gse/curation/schema.py`, `gse/api/encoder.py`. |
| **Written zero / silence / unresolved** | A written `0` is a reviewed middle row. `∅` is a proven definitionally silent coordinate and writes no row. An unresolved domain or abstention has not established either value; contradiction is a separate conflict. A silent bank enters neither dot products nor norms, and a partial profile compares only on jointly written, question-selected coordinates with coverage reported. | [Lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md); *What an <Object> Is* §§1.12, 7.3–7.6; `gse/curation/schema.py` shows the dense-storage limitation. |
| **Prime lexical unit and combinatorial profile** | A hand-defined prime is the smallest stable lexical content, but its spelling alone may have other senses. The prime record identifies the intended lexical unit by its coordinate/sign **and basic combinations, roles and valencies**. Those properties are part of the account of the primitive sense. | [Lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md); [founder words](LEXICON_FOUNDER_WORDS_2026-10-24.md) F088, F090–F091; [Goddard 2012](https://doi.org/10.1515/ling-2012-0022). |
| **Bank relation** | A reviewed rule about allowed combinations of bank states under a located reading and role. It is the basic construction operation, while a prime is the smallest stable lexical content. A prime's documented profile can inform a reusable law, but one exponent's behavior is not automatically universal. The relation is not another prime. | [Lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md); [founder words](LEXICON_FOUNDER_WORDS_2026-10-24.md) F088–F091. |
| **Empirical bank coherence** | The same English word anchors native observations from several datasets. Stable conditional relations, including joint conditions and exclusions, propose symbolic bank laws. Score the additional resolution after other information is known; retain counterexamples and recompute marginal value as laws change. A strong singleton correlation is not required for a useful conjunction. | [Lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md) §§5.1–5.2; founder words F101, F106–F111; [measured source-coherence report](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/coherence-2026-10-24/REPORT.md); *Significance Weighting* §§7b, 13–14. |
| **`NUMBER` / relative scale** | Rohan's GSE extension for hierarchical intensity among readings with an otherwise shared masked signed vector. It has no intrinsic meaning in any bank and no literal prime exponent; it is an operation, not a 73rd lexical prime or 59th signed coordinate. It preserves row presence, written zero, silence and polarity while changing the relative magnitude of selected active content. A lexical `MANY` or `VERY` remains lexical content when warranted. | [Lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md) §3; [founder words](LEXICON_FOUNDER_WORDS_2026-10-24.md) F095–F098; `gse/primitives_v2.py` currently registers the operation alongside primes. |
| **Joint bank-configuration resolution** | Native observations at different source and sense granularity restrict a shared feasible set of reading/role/bank configurations. NSM-style valency permits some combinations and rules out others. Dependent sources are not independent votes. Conditional collapse measures what each valid fact or joined premise set eliminates *now*; the model selects only among remaining typed configurations. | [Lexicon construction design](LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md). |
| **Linguistic derivation** | The trace records the tokens, operators, roles, and transformations that produced a typed reading. HDC geometry is a representation/composition channel; decisions use discrete structure, resolved keys, and the existing coherence selector. Cosine sense resonance is a guarded secondary reranker. | `gse/api/trace.py`, `gse/symbolic/grounding.py`, `gse/symbolic/predicates.py`; GSE proof packet §§1–11. |
| **World result** | An imported Sparse Wiki candidate and its signed, directional, banked path evidence. GSE preserves the source record and does not rebuild the world graph or silently count world-derived-from-GSE EPA as independent evidence. | Current transitional `gse/symbolic/genealogy_store.py`, `scripts/build_genealogy_db.py`; Sparse Wiki `BUILD_SPEC.md` SW-02–SW-05. |
| **Register / genre / prose style** | Register is a broad language-use regime; genre is a finer document kind; prose style describes realized surface choices. A label or profile is an observation with support and abstention, not a story truth. The trained router is Rohan's bounded tail. | `gse/discourse/profile.py`, `nested_router.py`, `infer.py`; `GSE_vision.md` IV-H/VI. |
| **SymbolicSpellCheck correctness** | Separate token validity, intended use in context, repair proposal, committed repair, and abstention. The preservation law protects recognized valid words and names; context can motivate suggestions without silently rewriting a legitimate token. | `gse/nlp/spellcheck.py`, `gse/nlp/spellcheck_entity.py`, `docs/SPELLCHECK.md`; proof packet §§5,12. |
| **Multi-scale document / cassette** | Word, sentence, nearby context, and document evidence are composed once. A cassette addresses one in-frame uncertainty using out-of-frame evidence; it commits on convergence or abstains, preserving a correct value. Intervention can affect one statement while the signal spans roughly ±2 lines. | `gse/cassettes/multiscale.py`, `deck.py`, `cheatsheet.py`; GSE `AGENTS.md`, Genesis cassette design. |
| **Literal face / expected-pattern signal** | GSE can emit literal structure, affect, and observed context/genre features. Janus's comparison and pragmatic/story interpretation are Regenesis reasoning work, with separate evidence for both faces. | `janus_two_faced_meaning.md`; Regenesis `BUILD_SPEC.md` RG-03. |
| **Abstention** | A named reason for an unresolved lexical, structural, referential, or contextual decision. `UNAVAILABLE` (resource missing) differs from `ABSENT` (resource inspected and no referent). Preserve partial structure. | `gse/symbolic/grounding.py`, `gse/discourse/profile.py`; proof packet §§13,15. |
