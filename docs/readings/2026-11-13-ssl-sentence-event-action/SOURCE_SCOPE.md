# Sentence benchmark and event–action scope

The frozen benchmark contains 24 authored ordinary-sentence probes. The labels identify a target surface token, a native definition ID where context supports one, and the few coordinates needed for the contrast. Null bank expectations are controls whose evidence leaves that coordinate open for this round. Null sense expectations require contextual abstention. Neither null denotes zero. Scores distinguish exact-definition contraction from whether the production encoder selects and emits the definition in context. The benchmark covers six classes and is a focused diagnostic, with no claim of representative corpus accuracy.

Release 012 left all 23 event and action targets open on their intended definitions. Its encoder selected one of the 15 intended event/action senses, the injection with a syringe. This motivates two source-backed proposals. The benchmark lemmas and the native sense groups of its entries are excluded from teaching and model audits. They remain in the rule application population. The same one teaching and two cross-source audit questions are available to each policy, with the frozen baseline, candidate families, and model shared by both policies.

- **HAPPEN for an explicit occurrence.** The defined noun denotes the event itself, including an occurrence that something undergoes, a change, or a social event. The saved NSM chart licenses *something happens* independently of an agent. The frame requires an event genus or equivalent occurrence construction at the head of one complete native noun gloss. It reserves modal possibilities, independent alternative genera, glosses with ambiguous attachment, and presence-only uses such as *the occurrence of yellow matter in the blood*. A place, participant, account, or result characterized by an event does not inherit the event's coordinate through that mention.
- **DO for an agentive intervention.** The exact noun gloss defines the act of acting on or arranging something, using a narrow reviewed set of intervention constructions such as delivering a blow, applying force, or putting an object in position. The NSM frame *someone does something* supplies the definitional test. The existing GSE `DO=+1 → HAPPEN=+1` convention propagates the event bank. This construction reserves passive or involuntary acts, indefinite mixed alternatives, and all generic *act of* forms outside the reviewed intervention vocabulary. Other banks remain governed by their own evidence.

The proposals use the same native definition evidence and guard adapter in population census, migration, and replay. WordNet aliases of a synset retain their individual records but count as one source sense group for disjointness. Native Wiktionary leaves require the exact single gloss and a non-derived source identity. The contrast controls include a projectile, a created work, a wound, an inactive state, and a person defined by arriving. See the [NSM chart](../../../data/external/references/nsm-primes-chart-v20-2022.txt) and [GSE category content](../../program/LEXICON_CONSTRUCTION_DESIGN.md#category-content).

Both candidates passed complete replay over 401,596 records with zero mismatches and zero reopenings. The closure arm logged one local context refusal before inference. Its identical request succeeded with the context increased from 65,536 to 98,304. The joint migration consumed the same two admissions and teaching receipts, with no additional inference. It assigned 241 previously open cells and resolved fourteen of the 23 held-out event and action cells on their exact definitions. The two runtime gains were DO and HAPPEN on the syringe sense of *injection*. Fourteen of the fifteen target senses still abstained in context. The ten banks reserved as open on participant, result, and state controls stayed open.
