Basis definitions can be selected for their constraint consequences alongside common-word work, without requiring all prime signatures before any residual call.
3. Complete the existing proposal -> causal warrant -> typed guard -> held-out direct audit -> versioned admission path. Counts prioritize hypotheses; they cannot approve rules. Preserve counterexamples and model provenance, and use the existing resolver for staged closure.
4. Implement historical rule-set migration: replay old model receipts against their original rules and state, then re-close the candidate under a new approved version. Invalidate/reconsider affected downstream model context with provenance rather than rewriting past receipts. Check whole-population changes before committing a migration.
5. Establish a source-backed independent semantic audit across the basis and relevant definition classes. Another model's agreement is useful evidence but not automatically independent gold. Use it with full replay, scope checks and completeness for eventual publication.
