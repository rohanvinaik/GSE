# Nondeictic physical naming: specification-learning round

This is a new measured phase under `gse.ssl-construction/2`, following the
singleton-only controller diagnosis. Its records belong beside the existing
lexicon experiment records; it is not a second construction design.

The founder's approved boundary is first-/second-person reference only;
generic selfhood does not supply I or YOU. The exact decision is retained in
`023-approved-person-reference-boundary.json`. The native closed-class I/YOU
definitions are positive controls. The approved generic self definition is an
outside-scope negative control, not an empirical audit result.

The candidate mechanism is own-word naming versus discourse deixis. An exact
native noun definition naming a physical non-person entity or kind does not
thereby refer to the current speaker or addressee. Under the approved bank
meanings its own content is orthogonal to I and YOU. Sentence-level reference
binding and any participant described in its gloss remain separate. This is a
positive type/role argument, not a zero supplied by a missing source field.

The initial sufficient scope is the conjunction of noun POS, an attested native
lemma, a witnessed WordNet `physical_entity.n.01` ancestry and witnessed exclusion
from `person.n.01` ancestry. Both ancestry predicates must be established from
the same exact definition's stored native paths. This deliberately reserves
person/address/pronominal definitions. It does not claim that physicality is a
necessary condition for nondeictic naming, or that every noun has zero deixis.
World associations, alias spellings and unrelated senses cannot activate it.

The first Zipf-aware teaching frontier consists of the exact ordinary water,
body and rock senses. Their source definitions must be inspected and their
eligibility established before frequency orders them. Aggregate spelling
frequency is labelled as such; it is not attributed to a rare homograph.
Holdouts span animals, plants, artifacts and matter, use distinct native senses
and spellings, and are disjoint from teaching. The proposed rules and their
answers are withheld from direct audit requests.

Stage each rule separately and together over the entire eligible population.
Retain exact contradictions, model-prefix reconsiderations, partial narrowing
and joint-space contraction. Independent model disagreement is a typed challenge
to inspect, not grounds for inventing an exception or voting a sign into place.
Admission requires the semantic warrant and successful independent audits;
model agreement alone is not semantic gold. If a counterexample changes the
guard, record the narrower mechanism and re-stage the entire scope.

Measure baseline, direct teaching and rule propagation separately, including
the full-lexicon frequency buckets. An accepted model prefix may be archived by
the existing rule migration when its context changes; reopened cells remain
unknown and are reported. Full replay is required before publication. This
round does not claim to complete the lexicon, all foundational laws, general
rule-universe discovery, or the common-language runtime checkpoint.
