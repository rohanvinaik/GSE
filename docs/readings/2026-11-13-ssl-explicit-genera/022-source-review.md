## Temporal extent warrant

The approved catalogue maps NSM TIME~WHEN to GSE WHEN. The chart explicitly distinguishes times, moments, and intervals of unspecified/short/long extent. A definition headed by an explicit period or interval of time therefore has its own temporal content. The source's exact qualifier may describe sleeping, employment, an orbit, fishing, a legal term, or childbirth without changing that temporal head. This supplies WHEN alone. It supplies neither a fixed duration, QUANTITY, LONG_TIME, BODY, BE, nor an event's participant roles. Those remain separately justified questions.

The rule does not identify the interval's location in a sentence's timeline. Existing sentence geometry owns attachment, temporal links and grammatical binding. The special `(FOR) SOME TIME` catalogue construction remains sentence-level and excluded from lexical assignments.

## Human collective warrant

The saved chart's PEOPLE unit denotes people collectively and permits quantification over these people. The existing primitive registry calls PEOPLE a collective substantive. A complete native noun definition headed by people or an explicitly human collection supplies this content directly. A lexicalized collective sense of *room* is distinct from its spatial sense. A plural group of dead people does not thereby become alive: PEOPLE supplies no ALIVE, BODY, first/second-person reference or desire assignment. A definition describing one person or an object used by people does not establish this collective head.
