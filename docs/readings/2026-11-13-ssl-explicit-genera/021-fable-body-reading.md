# Independent reading of the BODY disagreements

**Bottom line:** the packet contains no independent justification for BODY=0 on the literal bird gloss, and the gloss's own words meet the contract's literal positive condition without any appeal to inheritance, entity reference or animacy. The `teacher_man` 0 is a different case: on the supplied contract it is unsupported rather than contradicted. I issue no bank value.

## Evidence

**Contract (`typed_coordinate.scope_convention`, `typed_task_contract`)**
- Positive requires `DEFINITION_OWN_BODILY_CONTENT`, of type `ANATOMICAL_STRUCTURE` or `ANATOMICAL_SITE_REQUIRED_BY_DEFINITION`.
- `ENTITY_REFERENCE_ALONE` and `GRAMMATICAL_ANIMACY_ALONE` are listed only as insufficient for positive.
- Zero requires `INDEPENDENT_DEFINITION_SCOPED_JUSTIFICATION`; unsupported remains `UNRESOLVED`.
- The examples show that an anatomical term in non-head position suffices: "Delivered into a vein" gives 1, "…the chamber of a pump" gives 0.
- Scope is `OWN_DEFINITION`. Candidate links are "NOT_AUTOMATIC_ASSIGNMENT".

**Design section**
- The entity-reference scope is limited to "complete exact native reference-only pronoun records" and is "not… a negative BODY assertion, or a zero assignment".
- "Additional exact lexical evidence or non-reference content prevents that narrow classification."

## Interpretation

- **Explicit own anatomical structure is distinguished by both sources.** The contract names it as the positive condition. Goddard adds body content explicitly at each level rather than deriving it from [A].
- **Entity reference and animacy are distinguished only as non-warrants for +1.** Goddard's [A] corroborates that living, moving and feeling carry no body component. Neither source converts them into a zero licence.
- **Inherited vertebrate structure is not addressed by the contract.** No supplied rule carries BODY along a hypernym path, and Goddard restates body content per explication. Two cases differ here:
  - Bird and fish have "vertebrates" in their own text.
  - Snake, frog and man reach it only by path.
- **The bird 0 fits none of the supplied zero routes.**
  - The YOU rationale inverts, because the gloss itself is the anatomical description.
  - The insufficiency clauses and the adapter's silence leave the value open; they do not make it zero.
  - The man outcome cannot transfer, because the two glosses differ on exactly the contract-relevant feature.
- **A source-backed resolution exists for bird.** "Feathers", "forelimbs" and "wings" are `ANATOMICAL_STRUCTURE` in the definition's own words, in the same way as the vein example. Goddard [B] and Appendix B corroborate this. If that reading is not accepted, the contract's fallback is `UNRESOLVED`, not 0.
- **The man 0 is unsupported.** The gloss names no structure, so positive is not clearly licensed, and absence of a positive warrant is not orthogonality. "Erect carriage" is bodily configuration, which Goddard [G] treats under BODY.

## Unresolved conventions

1. **Organism-kind predication.** No contract example covers a noun whose gloss predicates parts of the kind's members; the examples are adjectives and the adapter covers nouns that are themselves anatomical structures.
2. **Genus-term content.** It is not stated whether "vertebrates" in own text contributes its definitional content as own content. The contract names `expansion_witness` (`/input/definition_content`) but its contents are not supplied.
3. **Hypernym-path inheritance.** No convention exists for snake, frog or man.
4. **Marginal bodily content.** The contract does not classify posture ("erect carriage"), physiology ("warm-blooded", "breathing through gills"), privatives ("limbless", "tailless") or hedged features ("usually having scales").
5. **`ARGUMENT_IDENTITY` and `WORLD_ASSOCIATION`.** Both are undefined in the packet. The vein example shows the first cannot exclude argument-position content generally.
6. **Mapping of sources to the bank.** `registry_gloss` for BODY is null. Goddard explicates English folk categories at "concept maximum", not WordNet's taxonomic glosses, so it corroborates but is not a GSE signature.
7. **Rationales for the two zeros.** The packet supplies neither one, nor whether they are independent of each other.
