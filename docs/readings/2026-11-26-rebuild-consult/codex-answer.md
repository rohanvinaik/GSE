# OpenSpec change: Rebuild the GSE lexicon around symbolic closure

**Decision:** Replace the current builder with one standalone, population-level builder. Preserve the only live database as the runtime fallback and comparison artifact while building its replacement. Do not carry forward old assignments as premises merely because they are in the release. The replacement must produce a substantially more complete **and** correct lexicon, not just a solver, a catalog of candidate rules, or a report on why cells remain empty.

This design uses only the supplied record. Founder instructions govern; the state section establishes what presently exists; quoted agent material is not independent authority. The storage layout, indexes, and scheduling below are proposed implementation choices, not claims about Rohan’s existing implementations or proofs.

## 1. What the builder computes

The builder encodes the **definitional contribution of a located reading**. The lexical atoms are the primes; the computational atoms are relations. In F088: “the true atomic is a *sub*-prime symbolic rule of relation between banks.” A bank is a coordinate, not a claim of statistical independence from every other coordinate. The coordinates are orthogonal; their **lawful joint configurations** need not be a Cartesian product. That is why closing one degree of freedom can close others.

There is one construction path:

```text
Native sources + authored foundation
  → located typed observations and alignment alternatives
  → shadow evidence ledger
  → population closure under bank relations and lexical inheritance
  → definition domains, derivations, conflicts, and residuals
  → warranted senses and construction records
  → existing sentence constraints and the typed GSE consumer boundary

Whole-population disagreement and residuals
  → candidate relation or censor
  → independent validation and marginal closure measurement
  → admitted rule version
  → population re-closure
```

The second loop is the SSL builder, not a separate tuple-mining job. An admitted law must actually remove alternatives or resolve readings in the real build. One-off additions are not a substitute for a reusable law; conversely, a warranted direct source fact need not be disguised as a law.

### Data model

| Record | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| **Definition / reading** | Stable ID tied to source edition and native record, lemma/form, POS, and reading identity. Keep head, argument, event, operator and construction bindings distinct. An uncertain cross-source match is an alternative, not a merge. |
| **Bank registry** | Versioned coordinate, admitted poles, aliases, interpretation and scope. F040 limits unpaired primes to `0/+1` among numeric values. Tier-0 definitional antonyms map through their declared polarity aliases. `INTENSITY` marks degree-bearing content; `NUMBER` is a separate relative-scale operation that preserves bank presence and signs (F098, F125–126). Do not infer scale from rating ranks. |
| **Cell** | `(reading_id, bank_id)` with a legal-state mask over `+1`, `−1`, `0`, and `SILENT`. A singleton is decided; a multi-state mask is unresolved; an empty feasible set is a conflict, not a value. Keep joint constraints too: equal marginal masks do not imply equal feasible configurations. |
| **Shadow evidence ledger** | Native value or exact locator, normalized assertion, source/version, dependency lineage, reading and role binding, projection rule, and disposition. Separate stated facts, authored declarations, derived consequences, candidates, and tail selections. Record why a claim was refused or left unbound as well as what was used. |
| **Relation** | Stable ID/version; typed guard and bindings; permitted or forbidden tuples over one or more banks or linked readings; authority, behavior tier and lifecycle; source loci, witnesses, counterexamples, and defeaters. A derivation names the rule application and its actual premises, not merely a rule that mentions the target bank. |
| **Sense / construction** | Lemma, POS, member definitions, warranted load-bearing signature, scale distinctions where applicable, binding and construction behavior, residuals, and provenance. The runtime consumes these records, not a flattened lemma-level union. |

F118 says “0 is explicit absence; empty is silence.” Thus a written zero is a determination, not a missing row. A **proved silent** cell is also decided (F140); **no observation** places neither zero nor silence in the artifact. If the sparse numeric projection represents silence by omitting a row, a decision mask or side record must distinguish it from unresolved. Likewise, a source’s orthogonality to a question is not itself a lexical zero. `−1` means the declared negative pole, not generic falsity or missing evidence.

### A. Project native observations before resolving meaning

Build the formal map F056 and F059 ask for: **native field → what it actually asserts → reading/role binding → permitted bank restriction or other disposition → warrant**. Each source family has both “may write” and “must not write” examples. The full source inventory is considered; not every source is entitled to write cells.

- **NSM chart, verified explications, and authored foundation:** Distinguish prime identity, lexical component, molecule use, semantic template, argument requirement, permission, and prohibition. Bind an explication to the intended reading; preserve role and negation scope when expanding a molecule. An allowed argument is not a component of the head. Reconstruct all 72 prime profiles against the bank laws, and examine the 37 reconstructed function-word records for lexical and construction behavior. A prime may have one signed content coordinate without being an unexamined one-cell vector. No closure from chart omission.
- **WordNet and Wiktionary:** Treat explicit POS, located sense IDs and native relation assertions as what they say, not as final semantics. A WordNet kind link proposes a typed inheritance path; Wiktionary `form_of` establishes the declared form relation, not every semantic property of its target. Neither a gloss-derived genus nor a WordNet verb category is an NSM assignment. WordNet is a bootstrap source, not a referee.
- **VerbNet, FrameNet and PropBank:** Preserve frames, roles, alternations and selection restrictions. Project only what an admitted rule establishes about the reading’s own predicate or construction. A moving participant, an instrument, or a human argument does not by itself put MOVE, BODY or SOMEONE into the head.
- **USAS, ConceptNet, SentiWordNet, NRC emotion/VAD, Lancaster and concreteness:** Inventory each native field and its permissible use. Categories, associations, classifier outputs and ratings may expose a gap or challenge an interpretation; none decides a bank by score, bin, rank, or presumed sense match. A separate, defensible definitional warrant is necessary. For example, association with disgust does not make disgust definitional to *furious* (F115).

Join by exact native identifiers, declared mappings, or a typed alignment proof. Lemma and POS can restrict candidates but cannot certify identity among same-POS homographs. Keep unmatched data and competing alignments in the ledger. Deduplicate shared evidentiary ancestry: two encodings of one observation are not independent support.

### B. Coherence is constraint composition, not voting

For each lawful reading/alignment, combine independently warranted constraints by intersection on **joint configurations**. Compatible observations can pin different coordinates and, through a relation, jointly eliminate a configuration neither eliminates alone. Duplicate restrictions do not gain force by repetition. Incompatibility produces a record naming the reading, banks, source roots and rule applications involved. Investigate wrong sense, wrong role, dependency, a misfiling, or an overbroad rule. Do not choose the bigger camp.

Use the hierarchy in F148: foundational laws; conditionally valid rules; and local violations with explicit treatment. A conditional rule does not override a foundational law or a binding source contradiction. Suspend the defeated application, retract its consequences, and solve from the remaining premises. Preserve independent support. An unresolved clash among authoritative premises remains visible. In particular, F14127’s “out-of-variance exception” calls for diagnosing the exception, not throwing out counterexamples to improve a fit.

### C. Find, test, and admit actual relations

Build two discovery paths feeding **one** admission mechanism:

1. **Compile NSM combinatorics into executable laws.** Translate sourced prime grammar, molecule decompositions and templates into explicit allowed or forbidden configurations: pole equivalences, head-scoped exclusions, valency restrictions, single-bank implications and conjunctive implications. A chart permission remains a permission unless there is a warrant for a stronger claim. F067’s evaluator/descriptor example is a test of scope: test the lexical construction it excludes, not a broad claim that evaluative and descriptive meanings can never occur in the same sentence. F073 requires a witness where two premises **together** remove an alternative neither removes alone.
2. **Find candidate mechanisms across the population.** Read the independently projected spine, not just already-filled vectors. Within typed reading scopes, build postings for native assertions and warranted bank states. For a proposed guarded `A ⇒ B`, retrieve exact sets of eligible antecedents, independently stated confirmations of `B`, explicit incompatible endpoints, and unknown endpoints. Unknowns are neither confirmations nor contradictions. Use the same operation for conjunctive antecedents and censors. Seed conjunctions from NSM templates, observed near-misses, and endpoints of actual derivation chains; propose reusable guarded links when a chain exposes a missing consequence. This is how population evidence finds a mechanism worth articulating, not how co-occurrence becomes law. Keep the discovery loci, dependency lineage and counterexamples. Use separately sourced or held-out witnesses for validation; do not recite the discovery rows as an independent test.

**Admission:** Compile the proposed law and test it against closure without the law. Require an expressible domain explanation, correct sense/head/role scope, both positive and must-not witnesses, and independent stated-spine confirmation for induced laws. For an induced censor require a sourced near-miss and the retained-plurality/falsifiability check in Negative Specification §9. For induced promotion generally retain lawful plurality at the applicable population/version-space level; this does not prohibit deciding a particular cell or an authored prime. A derived endpoint can propose a law, never confirm it. Do not enable derived-only promotion.

Compute current marginal effect, \(\kappa\), against **existing closure**: what does this rule newly make reachable or resolve that wasn’t available already? Report new exclusions as well as new singleton cells, and distinguish direct reach from downstream closure. Recompute after changes; overlapping warrants do not get double credit, and complementary rules can unlock a bridge. A redundant rule is not a coverage promotion. If confirmations and contradictions alternate, test a narrower, source-backed separating type. If no defensible type separates them, record oscillation and withhold; do not tune a filter, add lemma exceptions on a whim, or use `P ≥ .85` and support `≥ 40`. The rule-handoff record is the executable relation plus its warrant and exact effects, not an approval ceremony per cell.

### D. Use censors to remove alternatives; require grounding for positive meaning

A censor forbids a scoped configuration. If it excludes one sign, keep the other lawful states. Write `0` when explicit absence is warranted under the bank contract; mark `SILENT` when the definition is demonstrably silent. The measured valency rule is a concrete candidate: validate that the premise is about the reading’s own bearer and the target is the person-typed predicate before installing its zeros. Do not turn “no vote,” “not mentioned by the hub,” or “not obviously useful” into a value.

Rohan’s November correction is explicit: “a positive defition can never be assembled through pure exclusion or definition through negatives” (line 23191). A signed lexical assertion needs a positive definitional root: a located source component, a declared atomic value, or grounded content carried through a licensed entailment or inheritance. Enforce that separately from mask arithmetic. If elimination leaves only a signed candidate without that root, report the missing content warrant; do not publish the sign as decided. Conversely, take F043’s narrow FEEL and MOVE positives seriously: a reading’s own explicit affective denotatum or literal change-of-place predicate can supply content. There is no requirement that a second source repeat the same fact before a valid direct projection can work.

### E. Close over banks **and** readings

Apply typed relation constraints by removing values without support in permissible tuples, recording the cause of each removal. Propagate consequences into every dependent bank and every linked reading. Check joint consistency where local arc consistency is insufficient; on a bounded exact check that cannot decide, return a named residual rather than certify uniqueness. No inference from an inconsistent premise set.

Inheritance edges are typed and directional. Apply the reviewed inheritance rule to the **property that carries**, not to a whole parent vector. WordNet kind-of, synonymy, satellites, entailment and Wiktionary form links are candidate edge families, not interchangeable equality. Require the admissibility recorded in the state section: a spine value or an independently stated value in a kind, relation-specific scope, consistency under closure, and declared defeaters. A sourced *creatures* molecule giving `animal.n.01` MOVE and FEEL is a concrete regression witness; a child using a prime absent from the hub is a must-not witness against inheriting silence. A cycle of inheritance or bank equalities cannot create its own initial warrant.

With evidence, bindings and active rule versions frozen, domain elimination reaches an order-independent fixed point. Changes that retract a premise, defeater or rule start a new closure from valid roots in the affected region; they are not monotone additions. At the outer SSL level, audit the closed population, diagnose surprise, admit a new version, and re-close. An unchanged round with no admissible new reach is a structural stopping point **for that rule and source state**, not proof that the lexicon is complete. Detect repeated promotion/demotion states; do not claim the add-only termination proof for an oscillating rule library.

### F. Build senses for the sentence constraint system

Within a lemma and POS, collapse readings when their **resolved, load-bearing encodings are equal**, not nearby. Use the active rules and independently grounded constraints to establish which distinctions bear on the definition. Record that basis; do not elect the most frequent banks. Missing evidence is not equality. If equality is unproved, preserve alternatives and their members. Preserve at least one reading per represented POS and retain genuinely distinct homonyms within a POS. Keep warranted scale distinctions even when ternary signatures match.

A sense is the coherent definitional core, not a union of every incidental property of its member definitions. Any member-to-sense projection must retain the warrant and must not erase a consequential disagreement. `lock` is the acceptance case for recovering a few coherent readings from many entries without inventing a global “main sense.”

Sentence-side selection is the contextual use of these same constraints, not a separate frequency ranking step. Use the existing grammatical harness to restrict lexical alternatives and propagate role and binding constraints. In “The florbles are bigger than the blobbles,” grammar can constrain nounhood, number and comparison roles without inventing lexical definitions. Do not build a new clause parser to populate the lexicon. Proper names and jargon remain at their designated boundaries, rather than being forced into the common-word lexicon.

### G. Leave only a typed tail

Run the ordinary build with **zero model calls**. After closure, an explicitly enabled tail can select among a few typed, already grounded survivor configurations, or abstain. Pass reading and role IDs, relevant categorical facts, warrant IDs, and legal option IDs—not a gloss and a blank vector. Legal options can include `0` and silence when those are genuinely supported alternatives. Revalidate against current closure before accepting an answer; preserve model dependence in the receipt and propagate after selection. A model result is not a source vote, a new positive root, an applicability table, or confirmation for promotion.

Use disagreement with independent NSM material as diagnostic surprise. If a reference is used to construct a profile or rule, it is no longer independent evaluation for that item. Held-out reference answers must not leak into construction tasks or make the offered answer inevitable. Words without an explication can have legitimate residuals too. Diagnose missing source, missing mapping, conflict, missing law and genuine bounded choice separately; only the last is a construction selection task. If the tail is large, fix the structural gap rather than increase the call budget.

## 2. How a whole-lexicon round runs

The declared surface is **401,596 × 56 = 22,489,376 cells** over 67,173 lemmas. At 30–40 definitions/sec, the old per-definition SQL path takes about **2.8–3.7 hours for one pass**, before repeated epochs. Do not put that path inside a new outer loop.

- **Bulk ingest once per pinned snapshot.** Assign dense definition and bank IDs. Stage native facts and alignments in batches. Store legal-state masks in compact arrays (four bits per cell is about **11.2 MB** for the masks alone); keep sparse assertion, exception and derivation records in side tables. Keep proof roots and reverse dependencies by stable ID rather than duplicating full explanations for every cell.
- **Execute laws over sets.** Index typed guards, bank/domain states and lexical edges. Compile small relation tables once. Apply a rule across the eligible definition set with bitmap or column operations, or over a batch of typed edges for inheritance. Use changed-cell and changed-edge frontiers to enqueue dependent rules. Partition by ID for execution, not meaning: deliver cross-shard consequences and drain them before declaring closure. Use the same postings for population discovery. Do not enumerate \(4^{56}\) vectors or run an SQL resolver for each definition.
- **Make each epoch a full-population result.** Pin source and rule versions. Close the population, score and admit proposals against that snapshot, then replay the affected region and materialize the next full-population artifact. A delta replay may skip rows only under a sound dependency test against the **old closed domains and bindings**, not just stated source features. A new relation can remove a tuple even where its antecedent was not previously a singleton; use a conservative invalidation set when needed. Removals also follow reverse proof and rule dependencies. Compare optimized replay against a cold replay; if they disagree, the optimization is wrong.
- **Write in batches.** Export sense records and indexed warrants in one staged build. The receipt accounts for every definition and its disposition, including unchanged, partial, no applicable projection, conflict and solver limit. Record phase wall time, peak memory, edges/rules visited, frontier sizes, and output identity. Require reproducibility across worker counts and processing order. F148’s minutes-to-hours expectation is a performance objective, not a measured ETA.

There is a manifest discrepancy to reconcile: the four supplied source subtotals sum to **401,593**, three fewer than the stated total. Use the live total for sizing, but name and resolve the discrepancy in input accounting rather than silently assigning those rows a source.

## 3. What counts as progress, and what survives the reset

Report **correctness and closure together**, at both definition and runtime-sense levels.

| Measure | What to report |
|---|---|
| **Coverage by warrant** | Signed cells, warranted zeros, proved silences, unresolved domains, conflicts, and newly narrowed but not decided cells. Break down by bank, POS/family, source, and derivation path. Separately report how many runtime senses gain load-bearing content and which ordinary readings now resolve in sentences. |
| **Specification space** | For a declared scope, \(H=\log_2|V|\) for the lawful joint version space. Count exactly in tractable components; otherwise report labelled bounds, including the sum of log marginal domain sizes as an upper bound. Do not count conflict as zero entropy or sum across linked components as if independent. Replay a comparable \(H_0\); report \(\Delta H\) from direct sourced premises, structural consequences, and admitted population-derived laws, distinguishing exercised structural leverage from the remaining external-information need. Measure the bulk/tail curve; do not substitute percent-filled for SSL completeness or transplant the WSD knee. |
| **Two-sign correctness** | On untouched references and ordinary-prose witnesses, separately count false additions, omissions, polarity errors, wrong scope, and zero/silence errors. Treat unmentioned components of a reference as challenges, not automatically negatives. Report why gaps remain and what whole family each credible next law could change. Frequency may define an **audit cohort**, not a lexical decision. |

The supplied baselines are distinct: **~506,600 stored definition cells (2.3%)**; **1,770,913 measured closure decisions (7.9%), not installed**; **79,780 measured valency zeros, not installed**; **63,638 derived sub-kind cells**; and **149,883 nonzero values over 110,678 runtime senses**. Do not add those layers as if disjoint. Release 021’s **32.8M joint bits / 35.0M marginal bound** are historical, not a same-snapshot estimate for the rebuild. The 35-explication audit (**50 agreements, 7 contradictions, 305 missing, 11 both-sign uses**) points to both correctness and source-reach problems. Where there is no available source vote, re-voting cannot fill the gap: look for NSM composition, a defensible source extension or inheritance, or name the missing premise.

**Keep as inputs or evidence:** native source data; the 248 verified NSM records with their provenance; the chart; explicit founder rulings; reconstructed prime and function-word claims subject to reconstruction; consumer-facing behavior and tests that still express intent. Keep the sole live database intact until a replacement is qualified; use its stored values as comparison data unless a real warrant can be recovered. Recheck the 68 release relations individually, including the 46 inter-bank relations. Account for the 22 banks without inter-bank relations, without manufacturing edges to meet a quota.

**Reuse only bounded components that pass the new contract:** native parsers and applicable parts of `to_primitives`, `SOURCE_PRIMITIVE_SCOPE`, the four-state/arc-consistency kernel in `engine.py` as a reference or verified kernel, `ledger.py`’s guard and verdict machinery, reviewed head laws, valency and inheritance logic, and the existing sense and `clause_selection` integration. Keep the SSL controller code and receipts as a source of contracts and regression fixtures; there is no checkpoint or teaching DB to resume. Reuse parts only if they implement the new behavior; the new builder must not wrap `resolve_dictionary`, call an older builder as a fallback, or inherit old semantics through replay pins.

**Discard from active construction:** threshold-based coherence and its 648 proposals as a source of lexical writes; per-definition orchestration; unsupported type/applicability tables; default filling and hub-silence inheritance; the withdrawn classifier, gloss-genus, and verb-category conversions; and any model-output-as-fact path. Keep minimal diagnostic receipts where useful. Do not use the reset as permission to destroy raw records or unfinished experiments of unknown value. Keep live plus in-build artifacts, with a third copy only for a named checkpoint need. Once the replacement is better and consumable, switch the live pointer without a separate publication ritual.

Keep tests scoped to the subsystem being changed; run the end-to-end and full-population checks at integration. A passing replay establishes reproducibility, not that the replayed semantics were right.

## 5. Tensions and unresolved decisions

- **Empirical discovery versus statistical decision.** F108 says “pull out cross-dataset correlations that are stable”; F101 says “Not ‘statistical similarity’, but dynamic mechanism impute” and requires symbolic validation. November line 32981 rejects turning coherence into “some FUCKING statistical threshold.” Use exact scoped witness sets to find and challenge mechanisms, never a numerical cutoff to admit a value. Data Geometry’s similarity trajectories, confidence fusion, voting and conformity bonuses describe other instantiations, not GSE lexicon requirements. The actual Harmonizing rule-impute implementation is not in the supplied material.
- **Zero versus orthogonality.** Data Geometry calls `0` “transparency, not absence”; F118 says “0 is explicit absence; empty is silence.” Apply F118 at lexical serialization, and distinguish lexical assertions from an observation channel that has no opinion. The exact `0`/silence boundary for each bank still needs a bank-specific warrant. F049’s provisional model omission check is not authority for mass-zero filling after November’s “Others should NOT be automatically filled in with zeros” (line 19963).
- **How much the model does.** F047 says “I don't expect the lexicon to be substantially built with just symbolic rules.” F148 puts the model “ONLY [in] the long tail,” and November asks “Why is the *model* making decisions?” (line 14461). The supplied controlling brief resolves the architecture: symbolic bulk, separately authorized typed selection, no model-created evidence. The tail’s size and whether a model-selected survivor may be installed without further review are not specified numerically. F066–67’s estimates are not quotas.
- **Valence, atoms and the basis.** F057 sketches “‘Hatred’ is a noun without value attached”; November line 14127 instead says “ ‘Hatred’ is a negative emotion” even in neutral prose. The later ruling governs inherent felt valence, not sentence tenor or a blanket sentiment rule for nouns. F088’s one-content-bank image and F148’s rejection of writing off GOOD as just `[GOOD:+1]` are reconciled by checking the **full** profile, not by requiring extra positive components. The historical 72-to-58 account, Data Geometry’s older basis description, and the live 56-bank schema do not provide a verified migration map. Nor does quoted agent text settle every GSE-specific convention for reference, quantifiers or causal coordinates.
- **What makes a bank load-bearing.** November line 12247 supplies the equality criterion—“the same bank encodings across the ones that are of high salience, that are definitional across the load-bearing banks”—but not a complete executable predicate for which banks bear load in every family. Start with inspectable, rule-grounded distinctions; preserve alternatives where equality cannot be established. Do not substitute frequency, a learned salience score, or an agent’s guessed list.
- **Completeness is a requirement, not a promised fill count.** “A *full* lexicon, not one with 1-2 banks per word” (line 15912) and “coverage and correctness” (line 32585) exclude declaring victory on the present sparse scaffold. F045 also rejects caution “to the point of degeneracy.” But the record supplies neither a warranted eventual numerator nor a per-bank coverage target. The output contract is a full-population, runtime-usable artifact with all warranted content installed, measured structural gains, and explicit residuals. The fixed-graph SSL bounds do not guarantee the changing-rule loop’s yield: Significance Weighting §13 explicitly identifies bridges as diminishing-returns violations. Measure this graph and its source gaps rather than promising someone else’s knee or filling to a quota.
