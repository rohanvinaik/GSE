## 1. What Rohan wants

Rohan wants **one auditable symbolic builder that learns reusable constraints and applies them across the whole lexicon**. He wants a usable ordinary-language checkpoint first, then continued learning on the same production artifact. He does not want a sparse patch presented as completion, or a dense artifact made by guessing.

- **Start from what the sources actually say.** F056 asks, “What semantic degrees of freedom are pinned by every typed, categorical assertion in them”. F059 asks for “a rigorous symbolic map of warranted meaning,” including when evidence should “leave a bank empty where the definition of the word is definitionally silent.” F088 says the primitive operation is a “sub-prime symbolic rule of relation between banks”; F073 expressly wants both individual and **conjunctive** effects across banks.
- **Propagate positive identity through structure.** On Nov 25 at 07:48 (line 16531), he calls it “a constraint propogation problem, not a statistics one where you mine a database for a rrandom match with a hand-tuned arbitrary filter”. At 08:06 (16783), he says, “You can't define a thing only by what it's not.” On Nov 26 at 19:38 (30688), he explicitly calls for rules that decide banks “by other sources of information, like other banks,” calls sub-kinds the easiest tier, and says WordNet is a starting point, not the gold standard. At 16:27 (27602), he says the work in the NSM books “should be extended in ways that are relevant and useful.”
- **Use justified exclusions, not mass defaults.** On Nov 25 at 17:56 (19963), “the ones that are completely orthogonal to a valid definition of the word should be zeros. Others should NOT be automatically filled in with zeros.” At 18:06 (20110), confident zeros are “the CORE part” of the information-theoretic mechanism. F118 says “0 is explicit absence; empty is silence”; F140 says confirmed silence “is not uncertain or an unresolved degree of freedom.”
- **Build senses and sentence readings from the same constraints.** On Nov 25 at 01:53 (11903), “Grammar is a constraint harness”; the output is an encoded concept produced by rules that “constructively and destructively interfere.” At 03:28 (12247), definitions are the same sense if they “resolve to the same bank encodings” on the definitional, load-bearing banks. Preserve each represented POS and genuine homonyms, not a frequency-defined “main sense” (20:20, 20739). Sentence-side selection is not an arbitrarily separate step (20:56, 21109). Proper names and jargon are out of scope for this lexical mechanism (03:28, 12247).
- **Use teaching to change the graph, not just fill cells.** F148 asks for a base that fills “every rule-warranted cell” and makes disagreements with NSM a teaching signal. On Nov 25 at 12:59 (18428), he rejects “this bizarre tuple-hunting exercise” and “a pile of unconnected one-off assertions.” At 06:19 (15124), he asks for “A script I can audit”; at 06:26 (15141), an addition without “a rule behind it or a direct warrant” gets tossed. F064, F067, F069 and F078 describe a small model selecting from typed survivors **after** closure, not supplying a source vote or writing vectors.
- **Deliver at dictionary scale and make it work in the actual consumer.** On Nov 25 at 07:15 (15912), “a *full* lexicon, not one with 1-2 banks per word.” On Nov 26 at 20:44 (31718), he reiterates “several dozen million combinations of bank encodings and associations.” The engineering request is a “single, complete lexicon builder that works” (Nov 24, 01:06, 5332), with a clean typed consumer boundary and a “named” refusal where one is needed (18:08, 7953). The OpenSpec distinguishes the validated sparse checkpoint from whole-dictionary completion.

## 3. The gap, and why the gains have stayed small

**The work has demonstrated several propagation channels, but not a production loop that repeatedly learns and connects those channels.** The distinction is not primarily one of compute.

1. **The older base lacked positive premises and links.** The OpenSpec diagnosis records isolated definition solves, weak Wiktionary grounding, unattached word-level records, and a rule set mostly producing zeros or passing existing signs. It also records 648 discovered relations with no write authority. These are historical findings, not fresh counts for 022. The current NSM comparison still shows why a stricter agreement gate alone cannot fix the problem: in most of those gaps there is no vote to agree with.
2. **Each successful run has had a narrow, justified population.** An I/YOU family, a valency restriction, or a guarded inheritance relation can resolve thousands of cells and still leave most positive lexical content untouched. The historical I/YOU stage forecast 50,526 singletons; it did not establish a broad foundation. NSM molecules supply positive roots, but an explication needs exact sense, role and clause grounding before it can support a reusable rule. A component used with both signs is not one ambiguous vote to average.
3. **Discovery has not reliably changed the next decision.** The proposal records emitted chain candidates not rescheduled, audit failures withheld rather than refined, and singleton-oriented comparison in the production path. The working draft reports stronger joint-space fixtures and historical prototype receipts. Those do not prove the public runner consumes those signals end to end. Deleted DBs also mean an old head cannot simply be assumed resumable.
4. **The shortcuts either do not scale or do not establish meaning.** One model call per hole is linear work with no reusable proof. Mass zeroing creates apparent density without positive identity. Broad inheritance creates false positives unless the edge preserves the particular property. Held-out recurrence without a definitional warrant is not a rule. A full SQL resolution sweep at the reported 30–40 definitions/s is roughly **2.8–3.7 hours** serial, before discovery, counting, audit or I/O overhead. That is a sweep cost, not an ETA for semantic completion.

The missing deliverable is a closed feedback path: **source evidence → a warranted relation → population closure → independently grounded surprise → revised relation → replay → different next work**. Until that path is running, larger batches mostly repeat the same narrow experiment.

## 4. What would actually complete the build

### A. Compile and close a single typed constraint state

For each exact definition/source/role identity, maintain 56 finite-domain variables over `{−1, 0, +1, silence}`, with provenance, guarded joint relations and a separate representation for unresolved domains. An unresolved cell is **not** a fifth output value; it has multiple surviving possibilities. A contradiction is a diagnostic, not a lexical value. Store a sparse projection for runtime, but persist or reproducibly reconstruct the exclusions and joint relations that support it.

Compile two kinds of structure into that state:

- **Within a reading:** authored prime/function-word profiles, polarity aliases, argument and POS constraints, and admitted relations over bank tuples. Support exclusions, conjunctions and exhaustive obligations, not just forward sign copying. If a legal tuple is the only support for a remaining alternative, propagate that fact; do not take the converse or contrapositive of a defeasible inheritance rule by default.
- **Across readings:** exact native sense and role bindings; reviewed kind-of, synonym, satellite/head, form-of, derivation, entailment, cause and attribute-antonym links; grounded NSM template and molecule uses. Each relation needs a direction and a contract specifying **which consequences it preserves**. For example, the `creatures` explication supplies `MOVE` and `FEEL` to the grounded `animal.n.01` reading; a descendant gets a bank only if the typed sub-kind path preserves that definitional feature and no applicable defeater blocks it. Neither the whole vector nor the parent's silence travels by default. Use other native sources where their assertions support links; do not equate WordNet ancestry or a shared spelling with semantic identity.

Concretely, build indexed native-fact, edge, rule-instance and reverse-proof tables. Batch-load evidence, seed a deterministic work queue with changed domains and edges, and wake only affected rule instances and dependents. Evaluate guards against grounded facts, not against lack of contradiction. Use tuple support and joint satisfiability for coupled components; a local propagation fixed point is not proof that every surviving combination is legal. Record rival derivations on conflict. On revision, invalidate dependent proofs, reclose, and reopen only outcomes that lost all valid support. Use sharded evaluation for independent work, with a single versioned head transition and full-cohort replay for acceptance. Neither iteration over \(4^{56}\) vectors nor a per-cell SQL/model loop is the algorithm.

Then derive **sense views**, without deleting definition identities. Merge within lemma/POS only on positive evidence of equivalent load-bearing encodings; two blanks are not evidence of equivalence. Load-bearing means warranted against real alternatives and consequential for the reading or its dependent constraints, not a frequency or similarity score. Keep witnessed splits and recorded outliers; do not let an unresolved member glue incompatible groups together. Use the same bank and role constraints in sentence selection: satisfy grammatical/frame and contextual eligibility first, retain all lawful survivors, return their shared values and explicit alternatives. Test through `GSEEncoder`, including chemical `He` vs. pronoun *he*, argument binding, spatial relations and scope.

### B. Make promotion a production operation, not a candidate report

**Discover from the stated evidence and from failures of closure.** Compile the 248 verified explications into exact bindings, explicit components, role/condition restrictions and molecule dependencies where warranted. Compare those against the base *before* importing the audit target into the proof. Separate missing grounding, missing positive projection, opposing conclusions and sign/role mixtures. Use native frames, sense relations, and grounded NSM recurrences to propose shared templates. Mine proof paths for conjunctions and bridge candidates; use real near misses to propose guards or censors. A chain that merely restates its existing endpoints is a shortcut. A composition that applies in an independently warranted population lacking that chain can add information.

**Admit only independently confirmed mechanisms.** A candidate record should contain the relation and allowed/forbidden tuples, exact trigger population, definitional warrant, discovery and audit lineages, positive and MUST-NOT witnesses, guard and boundary cases, and current counterexamples. Keep the asserted evidence spine separate from derived facts in the schema: a candidate cannot supply the proof of its own target. Audit the boundary on independent readings, stage candidates separately and jointly, check compatibility with the prime/head laws and preserve lawful alternatives. Require genuine new support or joint restriction, not necessarily a singleton. Significance weighting here is **marginal downstream reach in the current graph**, including complementarity, not a confidence weight on a sign. Retained plurality prevents the promotion mechanism from manufacturing its own confirmation; it is not a ban on a warranted constraint settling a particular reading.

A near miss must change work: wrong sense → repair binding; unsupported projection → repair adapter; missing condition → refine the guard; genuine exception → retain a warranted reservation; unresolved opposition → preserve alternatives. Independently re-audit any changed claim, atomically migrate the rule set, and replay the eligible population and dependents. **Feed discoveries, challenges and changed reach into the next scheduling decision.** Distinguish external teaching, propagation under old laws, and corpus-induced rule promotion: a promoted bridge changes what subsequent teaching can reach. The fixed-coverage greedy theorem does not certify that growing graph.

**Teach a distinction that separates live alternatives.** Group work by competing mechanisms, guards and remaining joint domains. For each eligible question, simulate each legal answer under the current laws, and stage its effects on live hypotheses and downstream closure. Record per-answer joint-bit gain, resulting survivors, affected population and cost or unavailability. Choose a case that distinguishes genuine rivals, not merely the highest-degree hub; for example, use guaranteed gain across legal substantive answers and explicit boundary discrimination, with spelling frequency as application priority, not as a value oracle. Compare bridge portfolios under declared search limits. Recompute after every meaningful answer, promotion or retraction. Independent source assertions and reviewed boundary judgments can teach both “this holds” and “this alternative must not hold.” One such distinction can repair a projector or change a rule over an entire branch.

### C. Prove zeros and silence; bound the residual

- **A sign needs content warrant.** A positive identity or a warranted relation can establish a signed component, including a negative pole where the bank defines one. Excluding other identities does not by itself establish what the thing is.
- **Zero is a proved absence or orthogonality.** Establish the reading's identity and scope, then apply a typed law excluding the bank's contribution *and warranting zero rather than silence*. A person-typed role restriction may justify such a result in its reviewed scope; “noun,” “no source vote,” and “not mentioned by the hub” are not general zero laws.
- **Silence is an explicit censor outcome.** Prove that the definition is silent on that coordinate under a warranted rule or an applicable coverage contract. If both zero and silence remain legal, retain both. An ungrounded reading, unknown guard, unavailable solver result or null answer licenses neither. Negative specification supplies a distinct audit channel: check that wrong sign flips, forbidden tuples, overbroad rule applications and zero/silence substitutions are actually rejected by value assertions, not merely by a crash or timeout. The analogy does not itself provide lexical warrants or imply that Detective ran.

Only after closure and available corpus teaching should `residual.py` emit a bounded question: exact definition and bank, typed role/source context, allowed answer IDs and abstention, with no expected answer leaked. Validate request/head identity, membership and reclosure; preserve the receipt. **Legality is not truth.** Under the stricter no-model-write correction, use the selection as an analysis or review artifact; commit only with a separate source, admitted rule or explicit owner warrant. If direct typed adjudication is authorized for some residual class, make that precedence explicit and versioned; do not silently infer it from an API budget. No free-form rules, whole vectors, or model-as-second-vote. F066/F067's tiny-tail estimates are design aspirations, not quotas. Report separately evidence unavailable, search limited, awaiting audit or warrant, and genuinely external teaching required. An open bank with no warranted question is not a queued API call.

### D. Order of work: reuse the production path, then prove it ran

1. **Protect the live copy and establish a reproducible baseline.** Pin basis/cohort, sources, frequency bytes, laws, runtime version and evidence identities. Recover from surviving receipts only where head and event digests verify; otherwise start a named new epoch from the live release. Keep one working head and, after activation, one rollback. Build durable candidate, surprise, next-work and transition state with dependency-based invalidation; don't recreate a database per epoch.
2. **Install a replayable common-use foundation.** Use the existing `GSE/curation/lexicon_builder/` evidence/frame/identity adapters and `engine.py`/`resolution.py`. Integrate the four-state, profile, valency, molecule and sub-kind work into that path, verifying the measurement-only channels before promoting their outputs. Build missing exact NSM/template and native-role projections and the corpus-wide indexed worklists there. Reconcile approved conventions and multiple-sense attachment before asking again. Reuse archived 58-coordinate mechanisms only via tested adapters; no new constructor, registry or pointer.
3. **Exercise the public loop and wire what is missing.** Use `scripts/run_lexicon_epochs.py` and `epoch.py`; reuse `discovery.py`/`grounded_rules.py`, `completion.py`/`residual.py`/`model_protocol.py`/`reviewed_patterns.py`, the existing refinement path, and `rule_admission.py`/`rule_migration.py`/history. Build or verify the missing feedback seams: discovery intake, independent audit → refinement → fresh audit, joint revision and replay, current-state rescheduling, and a comparison function that consumes joint bits. First require the executable fixture to discover an undeclared rule, meet a genuine near miss, refine, re-audit, replay, choose again and resume after interruption. Then require real corpus receipts; a mechanical pass is not linguistic evidence.
4. **Pin the usable checkpoint as soon as it passes, then continue.** Apply the frozen common-use workload: 90% eligible token mass in both frequency and independent real-text aggregates, all senses retained, independent positive/MUST-NOT cases and explicit critical outcomes for promised operations. That's breadth, not 90% accuracy. Complete the applicable CP consumer witnesses, integrated checks and whole-candidate replay; use `publication.py`/`publish_round`, the existing resource pointer, and verify the exact installed artifact through normal consumers. A working pointer for live testing is not a certified checkpoint. Continue the same controller from the installed head and expand to every frequency stratum. No third build and no new discretionary approval gate.

### E. Measure joint bits, and report what remains

Run `specification.py` on fixed cohorts. For each definition \(w\), count the complete legal assignments \(|\Omega_t(w)|\), and report \(H_t=\sum_w\log_2|\Omega_t(w)|\) under the declared factorization. Factor disconnected components and free coordinates; if a live constraint couples definitions rather than just projecting across them, account for that coupling or qualify the factorization. Report exact-count coverage, bounds and unavailable components separately. An inconsistent component earns no contraction credit. A \(9\to3\) joint cut gains \(\log_2 3\) bits even when both marginals are unchanged. The historical I/YOU forecast is another check: its two isolated gains would overstate the joint gain by **1,457 bits**.

Save baseline, direct-teaching, old-law closure and post-revision measurements, with counterfactual replay to separate overlapping effects. Report actual budgets for teaching, audit, review and execution; compare arms on pinned inputs and equal actual budgets, not singleton counts. Report the required frequency buckets (1–500, 501–1500, 1501–5000, 5001+, unranked), retaining all senses and definition denominators, plus per-definition and spelling-weighted residual averages. Alongside bits report signs, zeros, confirmed silence, partial domains, contradictions, reopened decisions and runtime audit outcomes. Correct retraction may increase \(H\). This \(H\) is a log-count, not a minimum teaching-set size or semantic accuracy. An exponential bulk and a knee must be observed across rounds, not inferred from one large gain.

**The count to promise now is 22,489,376 cells accounted for, not 22,489,376 singleton values.** The first output forecast should be a reproducible per-bank and per-channel census of warranted decisions, remaining domains, and blockers, followed by realized replay deltas. The supplied evidence cannot establish a final fill count or overnight ETA. Whole-lexicon completion would require enough independently warranted positive and negative evidence to distinguish every intended residual that the representation promises to decide. Where the corpus cannot teach the distinction, an external warrant is required. Exhausting a bounded rule language or a budget is not completion.

### Conflicts that need an explicit contract ruling

- **Model role:** F064/F069/F078 and SSL-06 allow bounded adjudication; Nov 25 at 05:39–05:46 and the OpenSpec “No model writes a value” correction reject model inference as value authority. The 05:46 prompt also expressly rejects purging all previously authorized manual calls. The fail-closed interpretation is bounded selection without autonomous promotion, preserving earlier receipts and their actual authority.
- **Valence:** F057 uses *hatred* as a noun without attached evaluation; F066 distinguishes words with inherent emotive valence, and Nov 25 at 05:30 (14127) explicitly rules *hatred* negative. Do not extend the noun-wide GOOD=0 rule or infer intrinsic evaluation from contextual association. OpenSpec's earlier rating thresholds conflict with its later **no thresholds** correction and with the stated thesis. Ratings can flag an audit question; thresholds or agreement among ratings cannot decide lexical content here.
- **Sense attachment and revision:** OpenSpec's “main sense” wording conflicts with the later explicit rejection of a frequency default. Attach evidence only to positively grounded readings. Its “No stored value is ever changed” protects against silent overwrite if read as release immutability; taken literally across successors, it conflicts with mandatory revision and reopening. Preserve history and replay a justified successor.
- **Mathematics and release practice:** fixed-graph coverage and thresholded examples in the excerpts are not production sign-admission rules. The later joint-count contract supersedes marginal-only \(H\) and a static greedy guarantee for promotion. October's 58-coordinate discussion is not the live basis. The request to “just set the pointer” for live testing (Nov 25, 21:28, 21590) is in tension with checkpoint gates only if testing and certified publication are called the same thing. Label them separately.

The decisive test is not whether another family can fill 50,000 cells. It is whether an independently grounded discovery changes the shared rules, survives a contrasting case and full replay, changes the next question, and produces warranted meaning that the normal runtime can actually use.
