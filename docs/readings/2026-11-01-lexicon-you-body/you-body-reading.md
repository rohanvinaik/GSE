## Reading and reasoning

Read Goddard (2012), *Semantic primes, semantic molecules, semantic templates*, manuscript sections 1–6, appendix, notes and references. The saved PDF was read sequentially; the notes were reread completely after a truncated tool output. Section 1 treats primes as lexical-unit meanings with combinatorial properties. Section 3 explicitly introduces BODY in the explications of hands, children, legs, head and women. A person argument and that person's body occur as distinct components; a bodily component is not supplied merely by the presence of someone. Section 3 also describes number as a molecule contributing to measurement concepts. These are sources for interpretation, not a GSE sign matrix or a proof that independent primes have one-hot vectors.

Read Wierzbicka (2022), *I and Thou*, complete sections 1 and 3 of the saved text. Section 1 treats I/YOU as irreducible argument meanings rather than predicates or disposable indices. Section 3 distinguishes a physical pointing sign from the pronoun meaning it expresses: reported speech can refer to someone other than the signer, including the religious examples discussed there. This is evidence against deriving lexical bodily content from the mechanics of reference. It is not a metaphysical assertion about the referents. The complete article was not read; a whole-file attempt was truncated, and the present claim is confined to the complete sections read.

Read the complete GSE `sentence_architecture.md` and current `gse/lexicon/pronouns.py`, with references locating its builder consumer `gse/curation/external_sources/gse_closed_class.py`. The curated YOU record declares SOMEONE=+1 and second-person nominative animate grammar. GSE's word/sentence contract keeps reference identity and grammar separate from own-definition word content. This does not claim that NSM reduces YOU to SOMEONE: NSM's full meaning and GSE's word-scale projection are different objects.

The rejected inference is specifically: an animate person is referred to, therefore the pronoun encodes BODY=+1. That moves referent/world properties into a definitional bank. The narrow check recognizes the complete native reference-only catalog record, not the spelling YOU, pronoun POS alone, or the absence of a BODY token. Additional exact lexical evidence or non-reference content prevents this narrow classification. A bodily meaning such as hand, or a lexicalized body-related use with its own definition, is outside the check.
