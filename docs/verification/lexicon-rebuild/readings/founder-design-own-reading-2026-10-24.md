# Independent reading before the new Fable consultation

Date: 2026-10-24. Source of intent: the complete user submissions in `docs/program/LEXICON_FOUNDER_WORDS_2026-10-24.md`. This is my interpretation, not founder text or an implementation receipt.

## The distinction I previously missed

A prime is an atom of *lexical content*: the smallest stable thing this fixed vector basis can state. A rule relating two or more banks is an atom of *construction*: the smallest operation that changes which vectors are possible. These claims do not conflict. A letter is an indivisible symbol in an alphabet while spelling rules constrain which sequences form words; the spelling rules are not extra letters. Here a bank relation can be smaller than a prime word in the build process without becoming another coordinate in the lexicon.

F091 sharpens the atomic record. A prime's written exponent can have several ordinary senses. Its **basic combinatorial properties are part of the account of the intended primitive sense**, not optional metadata appended after a lone lit cell. The hand-reviewed seed should therefore identify the lexical unit, its coordinate/sign, permitted roles and valencies, and the combinations that distinguish that primitive use from the exponent's other readings. These properties can be compiled into bank/role relations, while the prime remains the smallest stable lexical content. A relation is an operation on possible definitions; it is not a newly discovered smaller lexical meaning.

The 14 signed opposite-pole aliases are exact identities of one coordinate. Other bank relations can be conditional and may reduce a state domain without selecting one sign. A one-prime reading is seeded by a hand-reviewed lexical axiom, then run through the bank laws. Its other banks cannot simply be declared silent by virtue of being atomic. A complex reading supplies several components, so both single-component consequences and rules that require their combination can fire. This is the mechanism of fast *definition* resolution. It says nothing by itself about whether the resulting word is unambiguous in a story: a rich vector can support more downstream inference chains.

## What a bank law must say

The proposed rule unit is a relation over typed roles and bank states, not a prose heuristic or source vote. It names: the located reading and its own denotatum/predicate/modifier slot; which source facts can establish the guard; the exact allowed or forbidden combinations of states; whether the consequence is a signed value, a written middle, proven silence, or merely a reduced domain; and positive and MUST-NOT witnesses. The same spelling in two sources is not a reading link. A property of an argument or referent is not automatically a component of the target word's definition.

Rohan's *box* versus *big* example is the test case for scope. The adjectival reading of *big* supplies a property requiring something to bear it; it does not itself denote an independent object. This can prohibit objectness in that modifier slot. It cannot globally prohibit a noun homograph, or prove every other bank silent. A bare POS label is helpful but only one fact among many source channels.

## Information and model use

The candidate space is a finite set of compatible reading, role, obligation, and bank-state configurations. One native source field can exclude many configurations; two independently scoped facts can intersect; a rule whose antecedent genuinely requires both can exclude further combinations. Repeated propagation may force a sign or silence bottom-up. Counting a product of 58 local options as the actual remaining uncertainty ignores dependencies. A valid source absence is a censor only where that field has a verified closed-world contract. Missing data is not 0 or silence.

The model is a bounded selector after symbolic reduction. Its input should be a typed record of categorical and numeric source fields, established components, and only remaining legal holes. Its output schema should prohibit all ruled-out states. A model fill is checked against source scope, bank laws, and omissions. No general clause parser or routine raw-gloss task prose belongs in this construction path.

## Research use and unresolved points

The official NSM chart supplies candidate prime forms, valency and allowable combinations, and the concept of semantic molecules. It does not establish GSE's 72-name/58-coordinate basis or a blanket cross-bank silence rule. FrameNet, VerbNet, PropBank, WordNet, USAS, Kaikki, SentiWordNet, NRC and other norms each assert at a different unit (sense, class, roleset, lemma/POS, lemma, association). Their documentation should become field-level contracts. A proposed hard rule needs both a real positive witness and a real counterexample that it must not mislabel. Some source fields will only rank candidate links. WordNet cannot be the master inventory.

Still to settle: the precise meaning of written 0 on unpaired rows; which candidate relations are universal, role-conditioned, or source-conditioned; how to certify a complete lexical obligation set without pretending an open-world gloss is exhaustive; and the projection of masks into GSE's current dense storage. These need explicit decisions or measured counterexamples, not defaults.

## Build shape I infer

Freeze/version the basis and source snapshots. Write and test the bank relation registry first, beginning with hand-reviewed prime readings and their closures. Build native source adapters and explicit same-reading links. Compile each located reading into constrained configurations, propagate to fixed point, present only the small residual to a constrained local model if needed, re-propagate, challenge omissions, and emit a mask-bearing vector with provenance. Prove the 72 prime names and a frozen random 2–10K cohort before the 1,000-word Zipf audit and full-worklist build. Audit false additions *and* false omissions. The existing old lexical scaffold must not serve as the implementation template.
