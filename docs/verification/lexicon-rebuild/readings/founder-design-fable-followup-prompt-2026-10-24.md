# Draft formal design to critique

# GSE lexicon construction: bank laws, prime words, and source-guided definition

Status: **formal design for GSE-01, not a report of a working builder**. This document supersedes `LEXICON_DEFINITION_RESOLUTION.md`. Its governing record is [Rohan's complete 2026-10-24 lexicon prompts](LEXICON_FOUNDER_WORDS_2026-10-24.md), cited below as `F001`–`F090`; later corrections in that record govern earlier sketches. GSE's existing source parsers and dense lexicon are implementation evidence to inspect, not the design's template. `docs/THEORY.md` is known invalid. This document deliberately contains no measured accuracy or completion claim.

## 1. The product and its boundary

For each **located lexical reading**—a particular word, part of speech, and sense—the builder emits the definition that GSE's fixed signed prime basis can express, with a mask and a derivation record. This is a word's *definition*, not its whole meaning. Grammar, discourse, world identity and facts, and Regenesis story inference remain separate inputs to meaning. `dog` and `cat` can legitimately have near-identical definitional coordinates while Sparse Wiki and context keep their referents distinct. The builder must not smuggle a referent's reputation, emotion association, or encyclopedic fact into a lexical sign. (F046, F055–F057, F059, F062)

Correctness has two equal sides. A false sign distorts the definition; omitting a warranted component distorts it too. A sparse result is correct only when its sparsity is justified. The output must report source absence, unresolved reading identity, basis-inexpressible content, conflict, and proven silence separately. Neither caution nor model fluency substitutes for this two-sided test. (F045–F047, F059, F066)

### Two different uncertainty spaces

The builder reduces uncertainty about **which definition vector is warranted**. A complex word often has several prime components, so more independent and combined constraints can identify its vector faster than the vector of an axiomatic prime word. That does **not** mean its use is easier to interpret. A richer vector can connect to more causal, emotional, social, temporal and world-grounded inference chains. Some of those chains can remain simultaneously plausible to different interlocutors. Definition resolution and downstream interpretation can therefore move in opposite directions. GSE must record the former without claiming the latter is settled; Regenesis and context handle the latter. This is the distinction Rohan made in F087. *Significance Weighting* is a source of insight about conditional coverage and inferential branching, not a borrowed numerical theorem for this lexicon.

## 2. What is atomic, and what is smaller than an atom

**Primes are the smallest stable lexical contents in this representation.** A prime reading is a hand-reviewed definition, like a letter in an alphabet. It is not inferred from WordNet, a dictionary gloss, or a model. The current stored GSE schema names 72 primes in eight banks and identifies 14 opposite-pole pairs, leaving 58 canonical coordinates. This is **GSE's** basis, not a claim that NSM research specifies exactly these names or this HDC encoding. The basis and its mapping to NSM research must be versioned and reviewed before a bulk build. (F072–F076, F083, F088)

**Relations among banks are the smallest construction operations.** They say which coordinate states can coexist, which require another bank or role, and which exclude another value. They are more basic to the *builder* than a word's prime vector, yet they are not extra lexical contents or extra coordinates. An alphabet has letters and rules for forming valid combinations; the rules do not become additional letters. Likewise, a 58-coordinate representation can have a much smaller lawful subset of the full Cartesian product. Linear independence of basis directions does not imply independence of admissible word definitions. This resolves the apparent contradiction in F088.

Goddard's account, which Rohan located in F090, names a closely related three-part structure: **semantic primes and their associated grammar**, plus **semantic molecules and semantic templates**. The relationship is useful but not an identity of implementations. GSE's sub-prime bank relations are closest to the associated *grammar*: they determine legal combinations but have no independent lexical vector. A **molecule** is on the other side of a prime—a reusable complex meaning made of simpler content, potentially nested. A **template** is a reusable shape for a family of definitions. GSE can study these to write and test relational laws and derived macros; it must not relabel a rule as a prime or assume an NSM molecule is already one of GSE's signed coordinates. [Goddard 2012](https://doi.org/10.1515/ling-2012-0022) supplies the research framing; F088–F090 supply the intended GSE construction.

There are two levels of relation:

- **Exact identity:** the 14 opposite-pole aliases are one signed coordinate, not two observations. `BAD:+1` is `GOOD:−1`. This quotient applies before any source fact and never counts twice. It is the simplest relation Rohan describes in F076.
- **Conditional bank laws:** under a proved reading/role guard, a state in one or several banks removes impossible states elsewhere. A single prime can trigger one such law. Several established components can trigger several laws **and** a new law requiring their combination. Unlike the alias identity, a law can leave two possibilities, apply to one slot but not another, or be inapplicable for an unaligned reading. (F073, F076, F088)

The atomic reading is seeded by hand with its exact prime content, sign, lexical role and an explicit claim of what the atom does and does not define. The bank laws then determine the consequences in the other banks. **Do not stamp all other 57 coordinates silent merely because this is an atom.** For each other coordinate the rulebook must establish a written zero, proven silence, or an unresolved state. If a reviewed law demands a second positive component for what was declared an indivisible atom, that is a contradiction to investigate in the law, scope, or basis; it is not silently resolved by the seed. A homographic non-atomic reading never inherits the atomic reading's closure.

## 3. The state being computed

The unit is a reading key `(surface, lemma, POS, sense/source locator)` plus typed lexical roles: what the word itself denotes or predicates, what it modifies, and which people/things/events are its arguments or members. The roles prevent a property of a participant, instrument, target, or referent from becoming a property of the word's own definition.

For each canonical coordinate, a *possible completed definition* has one state:

| State | Meaning in a completed candidate |
|---|---|
| `+1` | The prime content or positive pole is definitionally present. |
| `−1` | The negative pole is definitionally present; legal only on a bipolar coordinate. |
| `0` | The coordinate is applicable and a reviewed middle is written. It is not a default for missing information. |
| `∅` | The coordinate is definitionally silent; no HDC row is written. This requires a coverage or type proof. |

`UNRESOLVED` is the output when surviving candidates disagree about that state; `CONFLICT` means no candidate satisfies admitted hard facts and laws. Neither is a value in a completed candidate. An unpaired coordinate permits `0/+1` when written and may be silent; a bipolar coordinate permits `−1/0/+1` when written and may be silent. The **per-bank meaning of a written zero remains a rulebook decision** and must be specified before use; it must never be identified with silence. A silent row enters neither an HDC dot product nor a norm; comparisons must also report shared written coverage. The existing dense `primitive_values` table cannot represent this artifact faithfully without a mask-aware migration. (F040, F059–F060)

An internal candidate may hold multiple slots and relations before projection to the one vector. A signed property of an argument is retained in its role; it is not projected to the target word automatically. If distinct own-content slots demand incompatible signs on one coordinate and the fixed basis cannot represent both, report a conflict or a basis limitation with the roles intact. Do not cancel them to zero.

## 4. The bank-law registry comes first

Each rule is a versioned, reviewable relation over **allowed combinations**, not an instruction to cast a source vote. Its record contains:

`rule_id · source-of-law · scope/role · antecedent fields and their units · same-reading/link guard · allowed/forbidden tuples · exact consequence · whether absence is closed-world · source lineage · positive witness · MUST-NOT witness · review state`.

For example, the founder's *box* versus *big* distinction (F057, F088) yields a **candidate** law about an independently denoting slot. An adjectival modifier reading of *big* does not itself denote the object it modifies; *box* as an object-denoting noun does. The law must be phrased at the located reading and slot. It can remove `SOMETHING:+1` as an *independent denotatum of the adjective*. It cannot say that every spelling tagged ADJ is forever non-substantive, nor infer that every other coordinate is silent. Whether that excluded slot's final `SOMETHING` state is written `0` or `∅` requires the bank's own mask rule and an exhaustiveness proof. This is a real relational claim; POS is only one input to its guard. A substantivized adjective is a MUST-NOT witness for over-broad use.

The first registry must distinguish these operations:

| Operation | What a valid instance can do | What it cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| Opposite-pole identity | Canonicalize two names to one signed coordinate and shared write state. | Count an antonym as independent evidence. |
| Role/type exclusion | Remove an impossible state from one slot when that slot's kind is proved. | Erase other senses or slots of the spelling. |
| Dependency or required companion | Restrict a second bank when the first content requires its typed argument, bearer, or relation. | Copy an argument's sign onto the word. |
| Permitted combination | Keep a combination such as degree plus evaluation or quantity plus a substantive available. | Assert that every permitted component is present in a particular word. |
| Joint, guarded rule | Fire only when two or more components refer to the same located role; remove configurations neither fact removes alone. | Claim “conjunction” merely because two independent filters were applied. |
| Exhaustive obligation | Force a remaining realization when a named definitional component must be represented and all alternatives but one are impossible. | Treat an open dictionary, missing source, or blank field as an exhaustive inventory. |
| Source-link censor | Reject an incompatible POS, sense, argument orientation, or source record. | Turn a rejected candidate into the opposite prime sign. |

The rule engine begins with a **joint set of legal configurations** over reading links, roles, definitional obligations, realizations and coordinate states. A fact removes configurations that violate a reviewed law; changes can activate further laws, so propagation repeats to a fixed point. It must allow bottom-up inference: if every surviving configuration assigns the same value to a cell, that value follows even when no source directly wrote it. A rule may involve one bank, several banks, or a role relation across banks. It need not enumerate all `3^58` or `4^58` products; a factorized constraint representation can retain only active variables and exact relations. Products of separate cell-domain sizes are merely upper bounds on the real joint feasible set. (F058, F067, F069, F073–F076)

For every proposed law, print exactly which assignments it removes, with its guard and counterexample. If the guard is not proved for the target reading, it removes **nothing**. If a source is absent without a closed-world contract, it removes **nothing**. If all configurations disappear, emit `CONFLICT` and identify the incompatible premises rather than projecting a zero vector. No source's popularity or large numerical score can override a hard contradiction.

## 5. How research becomes a rule, rather than an appeal to authority

NSM supplies more than a list of base words. The [official English chart](https://nsm-approach.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/Chart-of-NSM-Semantic-Primes_English_v20_May-2022.pdf) gives examples of combinatorial syntax and explicit valency boundaries; [Goddard and Wierzbicka's account of primes and syntax](https://nsm-approach.net/archives/1522) treats combinatorial properties as part of the metalanguage. [Goddard's primes/molecules/templates paper](https://doi.org/10.1515/ling-2012-0022) supplies the larger lexical-typology method. An [author paper archived at ANU](https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/server/api/core/bitstreams/8bc1470c-e7b4-41a4-8161-2a10a1ed8b04/content) notes that prime meanings belong to lexical units, not whole lexemes, and that exponents can have variants. This directly supports sense-specific atomic records and research into legal prime combinations. It does **not** prove that GSE's eight banks, opposite-pole pairing, or every proposed cross-bank exclusion is NSM's own theory.

Research-to-rule procedure:

1. **Quote or locate the exact research claim** in a pinned primary source: a prime's valency, allowed frame, documented exclusion, semantic molecule, or lexical template. Record the source version and whether the claim concerns a lexical unit, a syntactic frame, a word class, or a whole semantic type. An example of a permitted phrase establishes *possibility*, not a universal positive assignment; an unlisted combination is not a prohibition unless the source says so.
2. **Translate it to GSE's state language.** Name the target role, source-level guard, exact allowed and forbidden bank states, and mask consequence. Record where GSE goes beyond NSM, such as signed size/evaluation poles or HDC silence. Do not silently equate an NSM phrase with a GSE coordinate.
3. **Find a positive reading and a MUST-NOT reading** in real sources. Test homographs, noun/verb shifts, metaphors, a property of an argument rather than the target, and a permitted co-occurrence. For the founder's noun/sentiment and evaluator/descriptor sketches, use the examples as hypotheses to refine—not a license for a global `NOUN → GOOD:∅` or global `GOOD excludes BIG` law. The official chart permits evaluation of things and degree with evaluation; the slot matters.
4. **Admit the narrowest surviving rule** to the registry, with provenance and review status. Let a rule stay a candidate when the research does not establish an exact consequence. Run it over a stratified lexical sample to find over-generalization, then specialize the guard rather than weakening correctness.
5. **Check the whole closure.** A law valid alone may contradict another law on a compound reading. Test single-prime seeds, pairs, and higher-order combinations, plus real non-atomic readings. Keep counterexamples in the regression corpus.

The same discipline applies to NLP datasets. Their documentation defines what a field encodes and at what granularity. The lexicon rulebook then states which combinations of fields and roles license a GSE state. The research is a source of **typed premises and conditional constraints**, not a precomputed set of word vectors. This is the Harmonizing/Triagegeist lesson Rohan identified: structure first, cheap selection only on the residual. (F014–F019, F057–F059)

| SentiWordNet | Synset-keyed positive, negative and objective scores can focus an *already aligned own-evaluative* alternative. [Project documentation](https://github.com/aesuli/SentiWordNet/blob/master/ReadMe.md). | An exact key can reject a false join. Score zero or polarity of a referent is not automatically `GOOD:0` or a lexical sign; it shares WordNet identity. |
| NRC emotion and VAD | Emotion associations and scaled valence/arousal/dominance can rank or challenge bounded affect alternatives when their units and scope are checked. [EmoLex report](https://doi.org/10.4224/21270984), [VAD source](https://saifmohammad.com/WebPages/nrc-vad.html). | Missing or zero association is not lexical negation. Lemma-level affect cannot censor every sense. Reject invalid local scales before use. Correlated NRC channels are not separate votes. |
| Concreteness and Lancaster norms | Human concreteness and sensorimotor ratings help distinguish referential/embodied candidate types after sense applicability is checked. [Concreteness study](https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/5774089), [Lancaster study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31832879/). | A rating is not direct `SOMETHING`, `BODY`, `SEE`, or `MOVE`; what users experience or do with a referent is not automatically definitional content. |
| ConceptNet | Directed, typed relations can propose a world or type path to Sparse Wiki and help distinguish referents. [Relation definitions](https://github.com/commonsense/conceptnet5/wiki/relations). | A world edge or its weight cannot write a GSE lexical prime. A negative relation applies only to that edge's contract; world facts remain the independent world channel. |

DeepNSM remains **silver evaluation and anchor material only** under the project decision: LLM-generated content and its license cannot be baked into the shipped lexicon. All source families require a rights ledger before any corpus export.

## 7. Construction path for one word, then every word

1. **Freeze inputs.** Pin the GSE basis and alias map, bank-law registry, source snapshots and licenses, worklist, model version (if used), and output schema. Preserve the original surface and frequency rank. The worklist is the declared universe; “full lexicon” means every in-scope entry and sense has a recorded outcome, not every possible English word has been invented.
2. **Route the surface.** Use explicit morphology and POS to produce lemma routes, retaining any independent surface reading. Open source-native candidate readings and their fields. Do not turn a word into one WordNet synset or a gloss bag.
3. **Build and test reading links.** Exact shared keys can join directly; other joins remain explicit alternatives until a certified combination of POS, morphology, source relation and role establishes identity. A word's spelling is not that certificate. Keep unavailable, absent and incompatible records distinct.
4. **Instantiate the bank laws.** Start with all legal configurations for this reading's roles and obligations; add hand-defined atomic seeds where applicable. Admit hard source facts only at their documented level. Each fact or conjunction removes the configurations it makes impossible; soft scores rank survivors without deleting them. Repeat until no domain changes. Print the change trail and any conflict.
5. **Read what is forced.** A coordinate is signed, written-zero or silent only when all surviving configurations agree and the derivation certifies the claim. Keep other cells and cross-cell relations as a named residual. The count of independent choices is measured *after* correlations, not guessed from the number of empty cells.
6. **Use a constrained local model only where needed.** Pass a structured record of source categories/numbers, reading and role IDs, fixed states, and a small set of remaining legal fields. Do not ask a prose question or send a raw gloss as the routine payload. Constrained decoding (JEV/Outlines-style) permits only the surviving enum choices. Re-run the symbolic laws on its fill; a schema-valid choice is a proposal, not proof. If the residual remains broad, improve a missing source/role rule or abstain rather than making a sub-10B model invent the full vector. (F047, F064, F069, F077–F080)
7. **Challenge both errors.** Check unsupported additions and missing warranted components across all banks and roles. A coverage certificate may close remaining states only when the relevant definitional obligations have been accounted for; provisional closure stays labeled. Preserve out-of-basis content. Emit the canonical 58-state vector, 14 alias views, mask, source/rule trace, unresolved alternatives and review status. Promote only under the declared quality gate and rights constraints; migrate to mask-aware storage before production use.

One word uses this exact path. A batch is the same path repeated over a frozen worklist with shared, versioned bank laws and source adapters—not a separate “scale” heuristic. Incremental checkpoints record both completed and unresolved readings. A rule change invalidates dependent outputs and rebuilds their closures; it does not leave stale vectors mixed with new ones.

### A plain example of the mechanism

Suppose a *particular reading* is certified as an adjective whose own content is a small-size property with intensification. The adjective-role law excludes independent objectness in its modifier slot. A signed size law writes `BIG:−1`, whose alias is `SMALL:+1`. A separately certified degree component writes `INTENSITY:+1`. The combination rules must keep size plus degree legal. These facts remove whole families of vectors; they do not by themselves prove every other bank silent. A property of an object merely *mentioned* in a source record would not pass the own-property guard. A real run must identify the actual sense records, show how their links were certified, and show the exact bank states eliminated at each step. This is a conditional example, not a claim about a completed source-backed word.

The reverse direction matters too. If a certified definitional obligation has exactly two legal realizations and a valid source field rules one out, the other follows even if no source directly wrote it. Remove the exhaustiveness certificate and the inference must reopen. That ablation is the operational test of the Sudoku principle in F073–F074. The more components a word truly has, the more such interactions may identify its **definition**; its later use in a story can still support more inference paths.

No toy candidate universe or source-presence tally passes. |
| Frozen random proof | A reproducible couple-dozen surfaces from ranks 2,000–10,000 (the existing fresh 24-item manifest is a candidate). | For every in-scope reading: native facts, link statuses, eliminated configurations, all 58 output states, residuals, conflict/unavailable ledger, and false-addition/omission review. Incomplete outputs remain explicitly partial. |

Detective should pin intent and negative specification at the real builder entry point: wrong-sense joins, noun/verb shifts, participant-property leakage, a valid rare reading, an open-world missing record, a written zero versus silence, an atomic homograph, and a compound that triggers a genuine two-antecedent law. Report identified, abstained, wrong, unavailable and partial by class; no single aggregate score hides a false-addition or false-omission failure. The priority is a functioning constructor and honest semantic evidence, not an elaborate performance gate before the core path works. (F008–F013, F070, F075, F083)

## 9. Decisions the rulebook must not fake

- **Unpaired zero:** the user approved `0/+1` for unpaired primes (F040), but the precise positive semantics of written `0` versus a silent bank still needs a bank-specific contract. Do not close unexamined cells to zero.
- **Noun sentiment and adjective objectness:** F057 supplies powerful examples of own-role versus associated meaning, and F088 makes bank relations the base operation. Those examples are a research and counterexample program, not permission to silently impose a global POS-to-sign table. Mark any broader law `CANDIDATE` until it survives contrary lexical readings.
- **Research versus adaptation:** the NSM prime inventory, grammar and molecules are research sources. GSE's 72-name basis, 14 signed aliases, eight storage banks, ternary signs, masks and HDC comparison are local choices. Record mappings and deviations explicitly.
- **Completeness:** no ordinary open-world source alone proves that all definitional obligations are listed. The omission challenge must identify a bounded unit and its coverage basis; otherwise output `PARTIAL` rather than a deceptively sparse “complete” vector.
- **Model authority:** a tiny residual is an empirical goal, not a promise. A model selection may finish a candidate record but cannot make an invalid source link or law true. Promotion criteria need measured error classes and review of the Zipf cohort.
- **Definition versus interpretation:** richer lexical encodings can improve vector identification and simultaneously widen downstream inference. Do not score GSE-01 by one “semantic clarity” number or feed world affect back as lexical GOOD/BAD.
