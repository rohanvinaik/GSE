## Agreement retained

- Fable wrote: “a prime is atomic as **content** (no stable smaller definition exists); a relation is atomic as **operation** (no smaller step of construction exists).” I retain that distinction as a reading of F088. Atomic seeds need relational closure, and all-other-silent-by-fiat is invalid.
- Exact opposite-pole aliases differ from conditional laws. A source's own level, reading identity, and role must be preserved.
- NSM combinability establishes permission; it does not automatically fill a word's cell. An unlisted combination is not a ban. Founder examples about POS and evaluation need scoped laws and MUST-NOT readings.
- Definition-vector uncertainty and downstream inference richness are different measurements.

## Fable claims not adopted

1. It says conjunctions should fire only when both antecedents are already resolved. That may be an optimization for a simple worklist, but it is not the *semantics* of a joint constraint. A relation `A ∧ B ⇒ C` forbids configurations with A and B true and C false even when all three cells are locally unresolved. Joint support and bottom-up forcing depend on this; the complete feasible set must retain the relation before any trigger is chosen.
2. It proposes an ablation where removing one source family must not change a cell certified by another. This would reject the founder's intended constructive interaction: a conclusion may require the combination of two independent source facts. A valid ablation instead removes a premise and predicts exactly which joint consequences reopen, while consequences with an independent complete proof remain.
3. It says a semantic molecule is out-of-basis and therefore no prime is forced. NSM molecules are composed meanings and may have prime decompositions; any genuinely inexpressible remainder must be recorded separately. A molecule is not automatically a silent bank.
4. It says an out-of-basis component makes affected prime cells silent. Inexpressible content and proved silence are different. The projection may have some entailed primes plus an out-of-basis remainder; open cells remain unresolved unless a law closes them.
5. It merges FrameNet, VerbNet, PropBank, WordNet, USAS and affective sources into a handful of broad 'independent families.' Some records share direct lineage or mappings, but broad similarity does not make their distinct field observations identical. Track exact lineage and conditional dependence rather than either counting every source as a vote or collapsing whole families to one observation.
6. Its worked *hate* sketch invents several unreviewed laws and then estimates only a few remaining calls. The sketch is useful as a hypothesis list, not an executable proof or empirical residual measurement. Its numeric estimate is not adopted.

## New founder evidence

F090 quotes Goddard (2012), *Semantic primes, semantic molecules, semantic templates*. The paper's described core includes primes **and their associated grammar**, with molecules and templates as additional analytical constructs. This strengthens the distinction the founder discovered: GSE's relation laws are most analogous to grammar, molecules are composed content, and templates organize families of compositions. The analogy does not establish that every GSE signed bank law is an NSM universal.

F091 adds Goddard's sharper point: an exponent's spelling can be polysemous, and the prime's basic combinatorial properties help identify the intended primitive sense. My revision makes the combinatorial profile part of the hand-authored lexical-unit record, while a reusable GSE bank law remains a separately reviewed rule with a scope. This is a reading of F091, not a claim that all GSE laws have been established by NSM.
