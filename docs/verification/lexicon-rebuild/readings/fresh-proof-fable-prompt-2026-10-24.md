# Resolving an objective lexical definition by joint constraints

Status: **the single active GSE-01 design**, revised 2026-10-24 after Rohan's tier-0/tier-1 and typed-hole corrections and [independent Fable readings](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/readings/typed-holes-fable-2026-10-24.md). Rohan's words govern. The code is not yet a complete implementation of this design. Dated receipts describe observed runs, not design authority. Do not use docs/THEORY.md.

## 1. What is being resolved

The unit of construction is a **located reading**: lemma, part of speech, source sense, and its own definitional role. Its output is the objective *definition* that GSE can express on its fixed basis. That is one contributor to meaning; grammar, sentence context, Sparse Wiki world facts, and Regenesis reasoning keep their own records. A source's opinion about a referent is not automatically a fact about the word's definition.

The current [schema](../../gse/curation/schema.py) stores 72 prime names in eight banks. Fourteen are opposite-pole names of 14 already counted coordinates, leaving 58 canonical coordinates: 44 unpaired coordinates allow lexical 0 or +1, and 14 bipolar coordinates allow −1, 0, or +1. The sign and the **write state** are separate. Written 0 asserts a reviewed middle; ∅ asserts proven definitional silence; UNRESOLVED and CONFLICT are construction states. No missing source record is a zero or ∅. A silent coordinate writes no HDC row and contributes neither norm nor dot product. Masked comparisons report shared-row coverage, following *What an <Object> Is* §§1.6, 1.12, 7.3–7.6. The existing dense primitive_values table fills missing cells with zero, so it cannot consume this artifact without a mask-aware migration.

### Tier 0: exact identity of opposite poles

GOOD and BAD are **two names for one signed coordinate**, not two observations and not two independently selectable definitions. BAD:+1 is exactly GOOD:−1; BAD:−1 is GOOD:+1. The mapping works in both directions, unconditionally. Written zero maps to written zero, and ∅ maps to ∅: the two names share the write state. The same quotient applies to all 14 pairs. It reduces the *representation* from 72 named positions to 58 degrees of freedom before a lexical source is consulted. Calling this an evidential or conditional propagation rule would double-count the same fact.

Every exact **atomic prime reading** has a hard-coded basis certificate: its one canonical coordinate takes the prime's sign and all other canonical coordinates are ∅ by the definition of an atom on this basis. The 14 opposite-pole prime readings select the negative sign of their canonical coordinate. This is the zero-order symbolic run, with no residual lexical choice. It applies to the atomic prime reading, not automatically to every homographic English surface sense. A prime used as one component of a non-atomic word asserts that component only; it does **not** import the atom's all-other-coordinates-silent closure. The current runtime registry also contains NUMBER beyond this stored 72-name basis; that drift must be resolved explicitly, not added by inference.

### Tier 1: exact conditional reduction, partial coverage

For a located non-atomic reading, a reviewed rule can remove impossible assignments in several banks under a **proved guard** about that reading's own definitional role, source alignment, and slot. Its soundness is exact: every removed assignment violates the guarded law. Its scope is incomplete: an unproved guard eliminates nothing; unnamed variables keep their domains; a named cell may retain two or more possible values. A conditional rule is about the word's definition at that reading, not a claim that sentence context or world facts have become lexical meaning.

This is the same informational move as tier 0 at a higher order, with a crucial difference. Tier 0 identifies names that are definitionally the same coordinate everywhere. Tier 1 narrows a **domain of possible states** only in the readings where its guard holds. Once a row is proved present, a bipolar sign may start at {−1, 0, +1}, narrow to {−1, +1}, and then to {−1}; an unpaired sign may narrow {0, +1} to {+1}. Other banks can narrow differently or remain untouched. Before row presence is proved, ∅ remains a distinct possibility; sign reduction alone does not certify silence or a written middle. Repeat all eligible reductions to a fixed point, because one narrowed domain can make another guard or coverage obligation decisive. The eight banks group storage and reporting; the actual rules bind cells, roles, and obligations across banks.

Correctness has two sides: do not add unsupported signs, and do not omit warranted definitional content. A complete candidate accounts for every admitted definitional obligation within the fixed basis, proves each written or silent coordinate, and survives an omission challenge. An out-of-basis lexical restriction, such as the bird-member restriction of *flock*, is recorded explicitly as such; it is not hallucinated as a prime or silently discarded. Source unavailable, reading unaligned, and inexpressible content are different states.

## 2. The Sudoku object: a joint feasible set

The solver's state is **not** 58 independent ternary questions and not eight bank questions. For one reading t, a complete candidate assignment names:

- R: target reading and any cross-source reading links;
- S: the target's own denotatum, predicate, argument and modifier slots, including arity;
- O: admitted definitional obligations from the located reading;
- M: one or more allowed realizations of each obligation, including an explicit out-of-basis route when warranted;
- P: every canonical prime's signed value and write state;
- provenance: the source fields and rule IDs that licensed the assignment.

The feasible set F(t) contains **joint assignments satisfying all reviewed laws and admitted hard facts**. It may be represented compactly, but the reference semantics is this set. For the same lexical content, duplicate source records do not create extra semantic possibilities. The diagnostic count uses distinct semantic assignments after quotienting duplicate evidence paths; source alignments and roles remain distinct when they can change future deductions. A raw product of cell domains is at most a diagnostic bound on vector projections, not the size of F.

Every prime decision filters F, potentially changing possible roles and cells in other banks. A prime's basis vector can be geometrically orthogonal to another while their **admissible assignments** are constrained. A domain D_E(x) is the set of states cell x takes among all members of F after evidence E. F is the reference semantics; a compact worklist of local domains and relations can implement much of the cascade. A singleton local domain is sound only if every relevant joint constraint has been accounted for. Nonempty local domains do not prove F nonempty: global conflict, bottom-up forcing, coverage, interaction witnesses, support counts, and omission checking may require joint reasoning beyond local arc consistency.

### The three mechanisms

1. **One assignment, cross-bank propagation.** A signed prime can remove configurations with incompatible roles elsewhere *because the reviewed initial laws already couple those roles*. For example, FEEL:+1 for the target's own affective content can eliminate pure size-description configurations; it does not by itself choose pleasant versus unpleasant affect or ban temporal content. A bare FEEL:+1 with no role and scope is insufficient for that elimination.
2. **Additive intersection.** Two independently valid facts filter the same joint set. F after both is the intersection of their surviving sets. A cell can become forced even if neither fact alone forces it. Under ordinary intersection, no configuration surviving both individual filters is additionally removed.
3. **Conjunctive rule.** A registered hyperedge has multiple antecedents and a shared-variable guard, such as the *same target reading and same own predicate*. Its consequent eliminates configurations that survive both antecedents considered separately. For facts e1 and e2, the interaction witness is the set of worlds surviving each individual filter and their ordinary intersection but eliminated when the guarded joint rule fires. A nonempty witness proves genuine conjunctive propagation. Merely observing a large combined log-count gain does not prove this mechanism.

After any elimination, re-evaluate every rule and global constraint to a fixed point. A conclusion may flow from a source fact to a prime, from a prime to other-bank roles, or **bottom-up** from eliminated configurations to a forced prime. A configuration choice is not required before a forced cell can be read: if every surviving world has the same cell state, that state is entailed. A guard must refer to semantic variables of the **same reading and role**; evidence location/review is the admission certificate for using that guard. A guard cannot depend on the transient label UNRESOLVED, because that would make a monotone reduction unfire later. If F is empty, report CONFLICT and the smallest available premise conflict; never convert it to a zero vector.

### Read one cascade in plain language

For the located adjective sense *tiny* whose gloss is “Very small,” a certified pure-modifier role removes independent SOMETHING-ness from that lexical slot. A certified own-size property makes the written BIG coordinate either +1 or −1, **not** 0. The source's *small* pole selects BIG:−1. At tier 0 that same state is SMALL:+1; SMALL is no extra evidence or dimension. A separate *very* component can write INTENSITY:+1. If a reviewer also certifies that this located sense contains only the size property and degree, the same-slot FEEL and GOOD rows may be proved silent. Without that coverage certificate they remain open. Nothing here closes the other 58-coordinate basis by default.

For *microscope* as “An optical instrument used for observing small objects,” the word denotes the instrument. *Small* describes its observed objects, so the **own-size** guard fails; BIG is not reduced. The instrument's user observes; SEE is not copied to the instrument. For *joyful* as “Feeling or causing joy,” the explicit two-way gloss can constrain a located reading to a feeling branch or a causing branch without assigning either FEEL or CAUSE alone. If a valid later fact excludes one branch, the other is forced; if an OTHER branch remains admissible, no such forcing occurs. These are conditional reductions, not an attempt to parse arbitrary clauses into a lexicon.

### The rule registry must contain actual laws

Each tier-1 rule is a versioned tuple: ID, same-reading/role scope, antecedent variables, allowed or forbidden assignments, source-level admission certificates, dependence/lineage, projection, and a positive example plus a MUST-NOT counterexample. It must say *which value is removed from which domain under which guard*. “May activate” is not a constraint; it eliminates nothing. The following distinguish executable narrow reductions from candidate relations that still need a reviewed consequent:

| Tier-1 relation | Conditional domain reduction | Boundary / present status |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed pure modifier role on the located reading | Remove SOMETHING:+1 as an independent denotatum **in that same lexical slot**; where the slot is certified exhaustive, SOMETHING is ∅ there. | POS alone cannot erase a substantivized reading or another slot. |
| Own affective-feeling denotatum | Remove FEEL:0 and FEEL:∅ for that own-content row, leaving FEEL:+1. | A participant's feeling, example, or lemma association is not the denotatum. FEEL does not choose GOOD or silence all temporal cells. |

NSM grouping is not the storage-bank partition.

## 3. When “one of these must fit somewhere” is licensed

Sudoku can force a value into a cell because its unit has **known exhaustive membership**, mutually exclusive placements, and a closed rule that the value occurs in the unit. Lexical sources are usually open-world. The solver may use a Sudoku-like move only when it carries a **coverage certificate** for a named unit:

- an admitted definitional obligation O with a located source span or reviewed typed reading;
- an explicitly enumerated finite set of admissible realizations, which may cross banks and may include an out-of-basis route;
- an AT_LEAST_ONE relation showing some realization must account for O;
- an EXACTLY_ONE or AT_MOST_ONE relation only when the semantic role really is exclusive;
- a validated source/sense/role scope, version, and a source-field closed-world contract if a non-event or absence is used.

If realizations A, B, C exhaust O, O must be realized, and A and B are ruled out, C is forced. If “unrepresented / other realization” remains admissible, C is **not** forced. Different sources can redundantly support one realization, and several primes can jointly realize one obligation, so “exactly one source” and “exactly one prime” are not general laws. GSE's 58-cell basis is closed; an individual dictionary gloss or data source is not automatically a closed description of a reading.

This is the precise content of Rohan's inverse Monty Hall analogy: the host's failure to open a door conveys information only under a known host protocol. NRC's zero emotion flag and a missing WordNet sense do not have that protocol. A source field with a declared, verified exhaustive inventory may support a narrow negative inference inside that field; the registry must say which field and what it covers. No global “absence means false” rule exists.

The completeness/omission challenge checks the **cross-bank obligation unit**, not each bank separately. It compares the admitted definition with realizations already accounted for. It can reopen a candidate, add a bounded residual choice, or certify that a reviewed configuration fully covers the admitted basis-expressible content. It cannot simply answer “nothing else” because a model was asked once. Unresolved content stays unresolved; provisional zeros and ∅ require their own derivations.

## 5. Information measure and the actual resolution loop

For a fixed canonical finite universe Ω, let F_E be the joint assignments satisfying hard premises E. The **logical entailment** of a cell value x is F_E nonempty and every member of F_E has x. A useful display is the support fraction

    support_fraction(x | E) = |{f in F_E : f has x}| / |F_E|.

It is a probability only under an expressly chosen uniform measure on those canonical assignments; it is **not** a measured probability of lexical truth. Logical forcing is the measure-independent 1-of-1 support condition. Conditional collapse from a valid new hard premise is

    gain(e | E) = log2 |F_E| - log2 |F_(E and e)|.

This is log support reduction, not empirical Shannon entropy. Its value depends on granularity; compare only within a fixed universe and report both counts. If F becomes empty, gain is undefined and the result is CONFLICT. Distinct evidence paths for the same semantic assignment do not multiply its count. Soft sources can rank configurations but do not appear in this hard-support gain.

This applies [Significance Weighting](<../../../rohan-vinaik.github.io/papers/Core Documents/SIGNIFICANCE_WEIGHTING.md>) as a **conditional coverage discipline**: one validated structural decision may settle a cluster; bridge premises can become informative only together; recompute after each step. The immediate observable is which domain values were removed, which became singleton, and which remained open. A sum of logs of local domain sizes is only a bound/diagnostic, not generally log|F|, because cells are coupled. It does not import that project's numeric constants, independence assumptions, or submodular guarantee. Fast collapse is not proof that a premise is true.

The loop for one target reading:

1. Resolve a frequency **surface** through explicit, POS-specific source morphology before loading the heavier banks. Kaikki `form-of` plus `form_of.word` routes an inflection to a lemma without reading its gloss. Retain an independent surface reading when the same spelling has non-form content. Keep multiple routes when multiple parts of speech are recorded; usage context may later select one. Then collect the 12 native source families for each resulting lemma and their states, and locate target readings. Version the basis, snapshots, field contracts, rule registry, and model. Do not make WordNet the master inventory.
2. Form a finite, role-typed candidate space and explicit definitional obligations. Establish source-reading links only by exact identity or a bounded, certified choice. Keep an OTHER/UNRESOLVED branch unless a coverage certificate rules it out.
3. Canonicalize all prime names by tier-0 identity. Add source facts and reviewed prime assignments through guarded tier-1 constraints; keep associations as soft. Propagate every unary, conjunctive, exclusivity and coverage relation to a fixed point. Ask both “what follows from this cell?” and “what cell is constant over all survivors?” An unproved guard, an unaligned source, and an open alternative remove nothing.
4. **Measure the symbolic path first.** If configurations remain distinct after fixed-point propagation, compile a machine-readable, partially filled lexical record: located reading identity; applicable source-native categorical and numeric fields with scope, units and lineage; fixed prime states; and only a small, named residual of writable prime cells with their surviving value domains and cross-cell constraints. The model sees this record, not an English question or a raw dictionary gloss. It fills only the exposed cells with a legal sign or ∅, using constrained decoding over the record's schema. No task prose supplies the decision. Validate the completed record against source applicability, hard relations, and negative specifications, then re-propagate. A syntactically valid fill is a proposal, not a warrant. If the residual is still broad or source alignment is unresolved, retain UNRESOLVED and improve the symbolic compiler; do not hand the whole basis to a model.
5. Run the cross-bank omission/coverage challenge. Project only entailed signs, written middles and proved silence to a mask-bearing candidate; emit the 14 aliases from the canonical states by tier-0 identity. Preserve residuals, conflicts and out-of-basis obligations. Export to an isolated mask-aware lexicon only after review and rights clearance. The current dense production database is not an export target.

The “often two or three residual model choices for common readings” claim is Rohan's empirical prior, to be measured. It is not a hard cap, a bank count, or a product of independent ternary calls. In the current build stage, run this loop **without any model** to measure the degree of freedom removed by source typing and symbolic propagation. Rohan expects a very small residual by analogy with TRIAGE's predominantly symbolic decisions; that is a hypothesis to test here, not a license to ask the model to do missing compilation. Raw gloss text is outside the routine model payload. An exceptional raw-text escalation would require a separately identified unresolved domain and an explicit warrant; it is not part of the normal lexicon path.

## 6. Required symbolic demonstration and build acceptance

The first executable demonstration must print the declared universe and counting rule; every hard premise with its source/level/reading link; each before/after survivor set and eliminated IDs; newly entailed cells **across banks**; fixed-point state under two premise orders; and a genuine conjunctive interaction witness. It must show a Sudoku unit whose exhaustive rule forces a value, then remove that rule and show the same local exclusions **do not** force it. A missing/open-world source must eliminate zero worlds. If a demonstration claims a full vector, it must print all 58 canonical write states and their derivations; fewer unresolved cells alone is not completeness.

The first [executable semantic-Sudoku receipt](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/SEMANTIC_SUDOKU_DEMO_2026-10-24.md) proves those propagation and ablation mechanics on a declared finite universe and reads real native records for one common non-atomic reading. Its source-alignment choices are explicitly assumed, and its remaining INTENSITY decision is unresolved. It is therefore **not** a completed word or a production vector.

The initial non-atomic reading cohort should exercise several primes and cross-bank propagation, with common senses such as an affective adjective and a directed affective verb where the native records actually support them. Literal prime words are useful zero-order rule checks, not the proof of lexicon construction. Add positive and MUST-NOT Detective intent tests: mismatched senses/POS, participant versus own predicate, source absence, unreviewed inheritance, contradictory premises, out-of-basis obligation, and removal of an exhaustive coverage certificate. Test the real CLI path, not only a pure helper.

The present [factor compiler](../../gse/curation/lexical_factor_compiler.py) is a partial prototype. Its real *hate.v* run established provisional FEEL:+1 and GOOD:−1, with BAD:+1 derived, but 56 canonical cells remained unresolved; it did **not** produce a complete definition. The live code also has older independent-cell, gloss-parser, and dense-zero paths. None becomes authoritative by being present. The next builder must replace that construction path with the joint-support and coverage contract above, while keeping native source readers.

The first runnable symbolic batch remains far from that acceptance point. With the expanded source-local registry and the narrow Kaikki link bridges, eight hand-picked lemmas emitted 111 eligible source-local candidates with 29 written cells. On 24 mid-frequency surfaces (25 lemma targets), the initial four conjunctions wrote zero semantic cells; the expanded registry and two link–frame–USAS relations wrote 52 cells across 493 candidates, with 85 modifier-silence cells. Thirty-seven of the 52 writes were WordNet-self readings, eight FrameNet-self, five VerbNet-self, and two Kaikki readings reached by guarded cross-source joins. The numbers describe rule coverage, not accuracy or completed definitions. Kaikki sense alignment and a coverage/omission certificate remain the dominant gaps. The dated receipt records commands and exact counts.

The first symbolic-only baseline and morphology correction are recorded in [the dated receipt](../verification/lexicon-rebuild/SYMBOLIC_ONLY_BASELINE_2026-10-24.md). On its frozen 24-surface mid-frequency sample, explicit form routing produced 29 distinct lemma targets. Source-family availability changed from 1 to 10 lemmas with FrameNet records, 0 to 6 with PropBank, and 0 to 5 with VerbNet; the denominators differ because the worklist expanded. This is upstream source-coverage recovery, not a correctness score. One FrameNet `Motion` candidate then wrote MOVE:+1 under the first typed rules. The remaining ambiguity is still broad.

Before a top-1,000 test, demonstrate the **symbolic-only** degree-of-freedom collapse on non-atomic readings, then complete and review several readings and inspect both false additions and omissions. The first model-facing fixture must contain no raw gloss or English task instruction, must expose only residual cells, and must reject changes to fixed or silent cells. Word frequency orders audit depth, not build coverage; measure the token mass of the audited cohort in an independent corpus and extend it beyond 90%. Then run the full declared worklist with stratified tail review. Report identified, abstained, wrong-sign, false-addition, false-omission, conflict, unavailable and incomplete cases **by configuration family and source coverage**. DeepNSM remains silver evaluation material only and is never baked into the shipped lexicon.
