## Typed-grammar limitations

The chart permission text is more complete than some separately authored slot lists. No formal grammar or executable interpretation of the colon-separated slot strings was established in this pass. Those lists must not be treated as exhaustive censors or used to infer silence from a missing slot:

- SEE and HEAR have no REQUIRES_ARGUMENT claim despite the basis naming their perceiver/content/location slots.
- SAY's typed list omits its documented topic; DO's omits instrument and companion. Their chart permission records retain those constructions.
- ABOVE and NEAR use the literal slot string place:reference, while their primary-chart permissions include someone above other people and near someone. This names the slot differently from those examples. It does not establish an executable PLACE-type restriction: no slot-string grammar or interpreting consumer was traced. Person references remain explicitly permitted in the profile. This is recorded as an unresolved metadata interpretation, not a demonstrated rejection or contradictory lexical cell.
- BE's generic typed list is specification-shaped; its separate construction_alternatives correctly supply the location branch. INFER and CERTAINTY require reasoner/epistemic-holder bindings in their construction metadata but do not repeat them in the short argument-profile list. These fields are not interchangeable exhaustive contracts.
- WORDS retains a stale until-WORDS-is-examined sentence despite EXAMINED_PROFILE. Its utterance:linguistic_content required-slot attribution is not demonstrated as a necessary argument by the chart examples or the written basis sense. The supplied examples establish permission, not necessity.

These are concrete representational/attribution limitations, not demonstrated wrong lexical cells in the current constructor. This pass did not trace a downstream typed-slot execution consumer or prove that it currently rejects one of these readings. Uniformly completing or explicitly marking partial typed declarations is separate from changing the stable signed profiles.
